## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Wells Fargo more than doubles its Bitcoin ETF stake to $2.35 billion

Its third-quarter 13F shows 49.6 million IBIT shares worth $2.35 billion at Sept. 30, up from 24.4 million, while its Strategy stake fell about 33% to 1.34 million shares. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/wells-fargo-ibit-stake-doubles-strategy-cut)

#### Goldman Sachs more than doubles its spot Bitcoin ETF holdings to $1.28 billion

Its third-quarter 13F shows $1.28 billion in spot Bitcoin ETFs at Sept. 30, up from about $580 million, led by a 52-fold jump in Fidelity's fund; its Strategy stake fell about 52%. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/goldman-sachs-bitcoin-etf-holdings)

#### Twenty One Capital buys the dip with 154 BTC for about $10 million

The company paid cash for 154.32 BTC as Bitcoin hovered below $65,000, lifting its treasury to 43,668 BTC. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/bought-dip-twenty-one-capital-adds-154-bitcoin-10m/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Coinkite CEO's remarks on $100M+ Coldcard thefts draw backlash

Rodolfo Novak (NVK), CEO and co-founder of Coldcard maker Coinkite, said he had dedicated 18 years to Bitcoin adoption and that about 0.17% of Coldcard users were affected by thefts that drained more than $100 million. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/coinkite-ceo-rubs-salt-100m-coldcard-bitcoin-theft-wound/)

#### duncan0k counts 35.6% of unspent BTC behind keys or scripts already public on chain

By duncan0k's classification rule, 7,164,754.12 of 20,109,207.36 BTC in unspent outputs at block 975,019 have an exposed key or script; the follow-up adds reuse counts requested in the BIP 362 review. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/how-much-btc-sits-behind-keys-or-scripts-already-on-chain-block-975-019/3088) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/standardizing-an-exposure-classification-for-existing-outputs-pre-bip/2866)

## Technology & Development

#### Henri shares a STARK proof of Bitcoin's UTXO set at block 972,500, with circuits proven in Lean

The proof verifies in about 0.5 s in a browser; most of the reported 72 s check is reading the 9.4 GB snapshot. It omits scripts, signatures, BIP68 relative locktimes and full sigop cost, so its guarantee is a bit weaker than assumevalid. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/a-stark-proof-of-bitcoins-utxo-set-at-block-972-500-with-circuits-proven-in-lean/3070#post_4) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/u/ajtowns)

#### Bitcoin Optech Newsletter #431 recap podcast

Murch, Gustavo Flores Echaiz and Mike Schmidt, joined by Antoine Poinsot, discuss the newsletter, including draft BIPs for anti-exfil signing and a block-processing stall. [bitcoinops.org](https://bitcoinops.org/en/podcast/2026/11/17/)

#### Draft BIP proposes a bip39() key expression for output script descriptors

#### COLDCARD Edge firmware 6.6.2X and 6.6.2QX adds Codex32 (BIP-93) wallets

Users can generate or import Codex32 wallets and split the active wallet into 2–9 Shamir shares; the release also tightens PSBT, key and policy validation. The corrected RNG implementation has received a third-party audit from Keylabs. [x.com](https://x.com/COLDCARDwallet/status/2122782501118902640) [coldcard.com](https://coldcard.com/security/status)
