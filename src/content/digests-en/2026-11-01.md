#### Bitcoin ETFs lose $643M after Binance hack, ending a four-day inflow run

Net outflows of $643.33 million on October 30 erased four straight days of inflows and pushed the week into negative territory. Five funds recorded withdrawals, with no inflows elsewhere to offset them. ## Security & Privacy

#### umbrelOS 2.0.1 security update addresses Linux kernel flaw CVE-2026-98432

x86 devices, including Umbrel Home and Pro, get a fixed kernel; on Raspberry Pi, where none is available yet, umbrelOS blocks the traffic needed to trigger the flaw. It also fixes a HEIC thumbnail decoder vulnerability and drops recently distrusted certificate authorities. [github.com](https://github.com/getumbrel/umbrel/releases/tag/2.0.1)

## Technology & Development

#### Developer Aaron Zhang tests eltoo on signet with TEMPLATEHASH, CSFS and INTERNALKEY

A third build of the same three-party channel after Inquisition 29.4 enabled OP\_TEMPLATEHASH: a deliberately published stale state was replaced by a newer update. The update script is 10 bytes with a constant 143-byte witness.
