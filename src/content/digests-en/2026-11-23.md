## Regulation & Policy

#### UK to ban crypto exchanges from making markets on their own platforms

Under the changing rules, firms making markets in spot Bitcoin on qualifying platforms are exempt from principal-dealing permission for that activity. Separate venue rules apply. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/uk-to-ban-crypto-exchanges-from-making-markets-on-their-own-platforms-as-bitcoin-rules-change/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Blockstream proposes a soft-fork path to protect Liquid's confidential amounts from quantum inflation

A large enough quantum computer could forge hidden Liquid amounts to create unseen coins. Blockstream's paper says every coin already carries data to check it under stricter rules, which a soft fork could activate if needed, with no user action or burned coins. [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2124660020478402781) [x.com](https://x.com/i/article/2124659661097885696)

#### Bisq v2.1.16 fixes Java4Shell vulnerability; update required to keep trading

The release updates the bundled Java 21 runtime to fix CVE-2026-114938, which could let an attacker run code on affected systems, and adds the latest Tor security release. Bisq urges updating even if you are not currently trading. [github.com](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq2/releases/tag/v2.1.16)

## Technology & Development

#### Haller-1: open-source AI models read handwritten BIP39 seed phrases, running experimentally on SeedSigner

@pizza\_bitcoiner's four small neural networks (714k parameters, 1.5 MB) run fully offline on a Raspberry Pi Zero, usually reading a photo in under 10 seconds. Weights, training recipes, datasets and the SeedSigner proof-of-concept are open source. [x.com](https://x.com/pizza_bitcoiner/status/2124553407159472599) [x.com](https://x.com/sesi_the_man/status/2124576930569822570)

#### Zawy floats a difficulty-adjustment change to tighten chain-length bounds under BIP-54 timewarp fixes

Zawy argues the DAA lags persistent hashrate growth, letting an attacker's chain gain blocks with less work. Shorter averaging windows help at a cost in stability; some DAAs could ease the problem by up to 50% at equal stability. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bounds-on-chain-length-with-bip-54-timewarp-fixes/2899?page=2#post_31)
