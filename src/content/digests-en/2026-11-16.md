## Security & Privacy

#### Luxor confirms hashrate hijack through its Commander platform, says attack was AI-enabled

Luxor says the incident was contained within two hours. CryptoSlate reports about 13 EH/s was diverted; an estimate of 64,000 affected miners remains unverified. Luxor's CEO warned crypto businesses of an AI 'dark forest'. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/luxor-confirms-bitcoin-mining-hack-and-reveals-ai-enabled-attack/) [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-mining-industry-rocked-by-ai-linked-breach-as-hackers-divert-13-eh-s-of-computing-power/)

#### Reported RoboSats Android flaw lets any co-installed app steal a robot's full identity

Per the report, an unvalidated Intent extra is injected into the app's WebView, exposing the robot token, Nostr private key and PGP key via a JS bridge. The researcher demonstrated the theft on the production v0.8.7 alpha APK, then the latest release. [github.com](https://github.com/RoboSats/robosats/security/advisories/GHSA-3jxq-v3c4-6j49)

#### Bitcoin mining difficulty drops 9.13% as hashprice sinks to $28–$30 per PH/s
