## Security & Privacy

#### BTCPay Server issues emergency update for 5 security flaws

According to the EU's cybersecurity service, the flaws could let attackers run malicious code remotely on vulnerable servers without logging in. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/btcpay-server-emergency-update-nginx-security-flaws)

#### Ledger expands wallet tampering probe to Berlin and Dubai

Physically altered Ledger wallets surfaced beyond Southeast Asia in the incident disclosed in October. Ledger found the modifications were designed to capture recovery phrases during setup and is offering factory inspections. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/ledger-expands-crypto-wallet-tampering-probe-to-berlin-and-dubai/)

#### Trezor launches Anonymous Pickup for ordering a hardware wallet without a name, phone, address or primary email

Orders ship in unbranded packaging to a local pickup point, with support, returns and warranty handled via order ID. Available first in most EU countries, more regions planned; paying with crypto also avoids sharing details with a payment processor. [x.com](https://x.com/Trezor/status/2123094478034510268) [x.com](https://x.com/i/article/2123091238941323264)

## Lightning & L2

#### Core Lightning v26.12rc1 release candidate is ready for testing

Spanning two release cycles and 832 commits, it brings smarter payment routing, HTLC recovery after channel state loss and splicing fixes. Developers ask users to try it on a test node before the final release. [x.com](https://x.com/Core_LN/status/2123328444272714160) [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2123330314147889476)

#### Lightning operators commit 603 BTC to new public channels, up 14% week over week

Per @ambosstech, operators opened fewer but larger channels in Nov 9–15 (1,396 vs 1,473; average 0.43 BTC vs 0.36). Forced closes fell from 1,723 to 357; public capacity held at 3,238 BTC on Nov 15. [x.com](https://x.com/ambosstech/status/2123129139192639661) [amboss.space](https://amboss.space/stats)

#### Testnet5 launches, giving miners a real trial of four proposed fixes

#### Bitcoin covenants, part 6: FE covenants and cryptographically restricted signing keys

FE covenants encode spending constraints into the signing key, so only transactions that satisfy a predefined condition can be signed. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/research/bitcoin-covenants-part-6-fe-covenants-enforcing-covenants-through-cryptographically-restricted-signing-keys)

#### Beulah Evanjalin proposes NestedMuSig2, a draft BIP for recursive MuSig2

The draft extends BIP327 to hierarchical signing groups, letting a tree of signers produce a single BIP340-compatible signature without revealing the internal structure. Feedback is sought on the spec and reference implementation.
