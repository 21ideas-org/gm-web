## Technology & Development

#### Anthony Towns weighs approaches to handling post-quantum signatures on the stack

Towns calls GSR's varops budget the right approach if scripts are to work with large signature data, sees an isolated signature stack as simplest otherwise, and rejects chunking, opcode blacklisting and optimized implementations. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/handling-pq-signatures-on-the-stack/3128#post_2) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/segwit-commitment-to-post-quantum-witness-data/2702)

#### Fedimint v0.13.1 fixes mint fee, startup and transport bugs

A drop-in upgrade from v0.13.0 with no consensus, API or database format changes: federations with non-default mint fees load again, and a fedimintd startup panic and iroh transport lockups are fixed. All v0.13.0 users should upgrade. [github.com](https://github.com/fedimint/fedimint/releases/tag/v0.13.1)

#### Blockstream app 3.7.0 adds Bluetooth Jade connections on desktop

The release also adds AMP2 account renaming and archiving and Ledger transaction review, blocks Jade Plus and Core units that fail the genuine check, redacts wallet data from support logs and raises the minimum Jade firmware to 1.0.42. [github.com](https://github.com/Blockstream/green_qt/releases/tag/release_3.7.0)

## Security & Privacy

#### Nunchuk 2.9.6 requires two inheritance keys to claim on-chain timelock plans

The change means no single compromised device can take the inheritance; optional Fallbacks can add a second heir or the owner's own recovery. The release also includes bug fixes. [github.com](https://github.com/nunchuk-io/nunchuk-desktop/releases/tag/2.9.6)
