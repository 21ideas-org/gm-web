## Security & Privacy

#### Bitcoin Core v31.1 released

The release fixes a path that could connect over clearnet even with the privacy network enabled for -privatebroadcast. Anyone using that option should update promptly. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bitcoin-core-v31-1-released/2696#post_2)

#### Bitcoin apps risk excessive fees as two library bugs are blamed

Applications that import PSBTs and rely on the vulnerable library's accounting can miscalculate miner fees and miss an excessive-fee warning. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-apps-risk-massive-fee-drain-and-2-library-bugs-are-blamed/)

#### Bitcoin Core updates its security policy for easy-to-discover vulnerabilities

Antoine Poinsot cited a rise in reports driven largely by LLMs. Issues flagged as easy to discover will get fixes backported to all maintained releases, released as soon as possible with a security advisory. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/1ehBdwhbU-1cUjU5SjRxn75XNUBvIfNbkfqPCJRFPlOfsBu5gHWotmNRNxcMirTl1baT2H-zBfJMeWM6rl-XJG8zV6gHf8vQ9b_TRB9vnIk=@antoinep.com/) [bitcoincore.org](https://bitcoincore.org/en/security-advisories)

#### OpenAI claims a cryptography breakthrough: does it threaten Bitcoin?

ETH here refers to the Exponential Time Hypothesis, and a theoretical SAT speed-up alone does not establish a practical cryptographic attack. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/openai-claims-a-math-breakthrough-does-that-threaten-bitcoin/)

## Lightning & L2

#### Lightning tightens invoice rules after a reported attack made Bitcoin withdrawals look unpaid

BOLT11 now requires one payment hash and one payment secret, and operators must check how their software records withdrawals. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/lightning-tightens-invoice-rules-after-reported-attack-made-bitcoin-withdrawals-look-unpaid/)

#### Eclair v0.14.6 patch release brings security hardening fixes

Upgrading is highly recommended, as some of the issues can be exploited by malicious nodes. A new setting controls whether splices can spend unconfirmed inputs (off by default); the release is compatible with previous versions. [github.com](https://github.com/ACINQ/eclair/releases/tag/v0.14.6)

#### Liquid Federation says BTC<>LBTC swaps were re-enabled as part of a staged recovery

Swaps run 1:1 via participating providers that handled 99% of pre-incident peg-out volume. Federation peg-outs stay under enhanced controls as an independent audit nears completion; full 1:1 BTC backing for LBTC is not yet restored. [x.com](https://x.com/Liquid_BTC/status/2115201117789245837) [x.com](https://x.com/AquaBitcoin/status/2115210194133156225)

#### Bessent brings a Plan B for the stalled CLARITY Act

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told Ripple's Swell conference that the Treasury can work with the SEC and CFTC to fill the gap left by the Clarity Act's failure in the Senate. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/bessent-clarity-act-plan-b-ripple-swell/)

## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin Knots v29.4.3.knots20260508 extends its long coinbase maturity softfork

Nodes and miners should upgrade before block 979919: from block 980019, a 29.4.2 node may accept blocks this release rejects. A pruned node that upgrades late may need to re-sync from scratch. [github.com](https://github.com/bitcoinknots/bitcoin/releases/tag/v29.4.3.knots20260508)

#### The long road to bitcoin payments: why adoption lags, and a call for moonshot ideas

@ConorOkus argues the gap is mostly demand, not technology: Lightning runs in production and more than a million US merchants can accept bitcoin via Square, yet few people pay with it in everyday life.
