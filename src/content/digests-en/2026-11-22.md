## Security & Privacy

#### COLDCARD publishes independent Keylabs review of its seed-generation fix

COLDCARD says Keylabs found the fix sound within scope after reviewing source and signed binaries of Mk5 5.6.2/5.6.3 and Q 1.5.2Q/1.5.3Q. The bug swapped the hardware RNG for a software one; migration guidance is unchanged and seeds made on vulnerable firmware are not repaired. [blog.coinkite.com](https://blog.coinkite.com/keylabs-coldcard-seed-generation-review/) [keylabs.io](https://keylabs.io/)

#### IOSCO warns of quantum risk to blockchains as Bitcoin developers weigh fixes

In its latest quantum computing report, IOSCO says networks including Bitcoin may struggle to prepare for a post-quantum world because upgrades require coordinated action. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/iosco-warns-quantum-risk-blockchains-bitcoin-devs-weigh-fixes/)

## Mining

#### Bitcoin mining profits turn ugly despite hashrate's return above 1 ZH/s

After shedding about 100 EH/s as the price fell to levels last seen in the first week of September 2024, hashrate reclaimed the 1,000 EH/s mark, equivalent to 1 ZH/s. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/bitcoin-mining-profits-turn-ugly-despite-hashrates-return-above-1-zh-s/)

#### Fewer Bitcoin mining hardware sellers in 2026 as BTC fell and AI grew

Companies that tried to challenge Bitmain's dominance have stopped selling to outside clients or sharply cut sales, pausing efforts relevant to Bitcoin's resilience. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/opinion-and-analysis/bitcoin-miners-fewer-hardware-sellers-2026-btc-tanked-ai-grew/)

#### Bitfarms paid an $11.9M tariff bill four years after delivery of its miners

Bitfarms believed the machines it brought into Washington state in 2021 were made in Malaysia, as its supplier said, and gave US Customs supporting documents; four years later it paid the government $11.9 million. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/one-bitcoin-miner-paid-an-11-9m-tariff-bill-four-years-after-delivery/)

## Technology & Development

#### Draft Multisig Session Protocol for remote multisig setup and signing seeks review

Keylay's developer proposes a spec for remote cosigners to exchange keys and PSBTs over a relay treated as hostile, authenticated by a short out-of-band code and a 12-character check, so no coordinator distributes the descriptor. Draft v0.1 supports two clients at a time. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/multisig-session-protocol-draft-spec-for-remote-multisig-setup-and-signing-needs-review/3103) [keylay.org](https://keylay.org/)

#### Cory Fields clarifies the 'quantum landmines' idea on the bitcoindev list

Replying to conduition, Fields explains that landmines would be new UTXOs holding hidden proofs of signing ability for other UTXOs, so an owner of old coins could reveal such a proof if a quantum attacker tried to steal them. The idea would require a soft fork. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/ffd99327-f633-4e2e-bc54-cc1389ffebccn@googlegroups.com/) [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/ffd99327-f633-4e2e-bc54-cc1389ffebccn@googlegroups.com/1.1-a.txt)

#### StartOS v0.4.3-rc.3 release candidate adds shared volumes and UPS monitoring

The prerelease lets services share local folders, external drives and CIFS shares with per-service access grants, can shut down automatically on low UPS battery, and syncs registries over encrypted WebSockets. It also includes security hardening. [github.com](https://github.com/Start9Labs/start-technologies/releases/tag/start-os/v0.4.3-rc.3)
