## Lightning & L2

#### Dave Lund proposes a Lightning channel option toward K-of-N nodes

The option\_independent\_secrets add-on (bits 266/267) drops the shachain requirement so signers can jointly generate per-commitment secrets. Prototyped in lnd and Core Lightning forks on BLAKE2b Bitcoin, not SHA256d Bitcoin; he says the code should carry over largely as is. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/towards-a-k-of-n-lightning-network-node/2395#post_7) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-8825-how-it-differs-from-the-original-proposal-1)
