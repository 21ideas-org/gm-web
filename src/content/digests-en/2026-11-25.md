## Security & Privacy

#### Coldcard postmortem says weak seeds, not hacked devices, drove Bitcoin thefts

Coinkite's full postmortem says a firmware flaw spanning five years weakened wallet seeds, and that about 242 customer accounts have reported losses. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/coldcard-postmortem-seed-flaw)

#### ELIP-352 for Silent Payments on Liquid merged

JAN3 announced the merge, crediting @FPupusas for the work and calling it another step forward for financial privacy. [x.com](https://x.com/JAN3com/status/2125333981117689973) [x.com](https://x.com/AquaBitcoin/status/2125341356226642262)

## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Over 2,200 institutions held $29.8 billion in spot Bitcoin ETFs at end of Q3

Bloomberg Intelligence counted 2,223 filers. Investment advisers made up about 84% of them and accounted for $13.4 billion in exposure. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/over-2200-institutions-held-29-8b-in-bitcoin-etfs-advisers-led/)

#### Bitcoin lender Ledn moves US business onshore with custody accounts launch

The accounts let eligible US customers buy, sell, swap and hold digital and traditional assets, expanding Ledn beyond crypto-backed loans. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/bitcoin-lender-ledn-moves-us-business-onshore-custody-accounts/)

## Funds & ETFs

#### BlackRock drives Bitcoin ETFs to worst month on record as crypto fund withdrawals near $6 billion

US spot Bitcoin funds passed June's monthly record on November 23, and BlackRock's Ether funds also recorded net withdrawals through that date. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/blackrock-drives-bitcoin-etfs-to-worst-month-on-record-as-crypto-fund-withdrawals-near-6-billion/)

#### UniCredit opens Bitcoin to Italian savers, capping gains at 65%

The bank began selling retail clients in Italy a six-year, capital-protected certificate linked to BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin ETP. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/unicredit-bitcoin-certificate-italy)

#### Anchorage Digital sets up asset manager to offer bitcoin yield to clients

The subsidiary, 61st Parallel Asset Management, is expected to open by early December with a bitcoin-denominated fund that lets custody clients earn yield. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/anchorage-digital-bitcoin-yield-asset-manager)

#### Strike offers businesses 3.6% interest on cash, paid in bitcoin

Interest requires enrollment and applies to eligible customers' USD held at Cross River Bank, FDIC-insured up to $250,000; the rate is subject to change. [x.com](https://x.com/Strike/status/2125275603137962256) [x.com](https://x.com/Strike/status/2125275600810357130)

## Technology & Development

#### First security analysis published for SHRINCS post-quantum signature proposal

Mikhail Kudinov shared the papers: a full spec and EU-CMA proof for SHRINCS, which pairs 324-byte stateful signatures with a stateless SLH-DSA fallback under one key. Two side results also apply to SPHINCS+ and SLH-DSA. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/shrincs-324-byte-stateful-post-quantum-signatures-with-static-backups/2158?page=2#post_23) [github.com](https://github.com/SHRINCS/Security-Analysis)

#### Sparrow Wallet 2.5.7 improves syncing with slow Electrum servers

The release upgrades bundled Tor to v0.4.9.16, adds testnet5 support and refuses non-standard descriptors on import; Bitcoin Core before v24 must be upgraded to connect over https. [github.com](https://github.com/sparrowwallet/sparrow/releases/tag/2.5.7)

#### Researchers unveil Praxis, a witness encryption scheme with potential Bitcoin uses

Stanford University and Byzantine Research designed it to make witness encryption practical on ordinary computers; it could potentially enable Bitcoin bridges and conditional payments. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/technology/researchers-unveil-encryption-breakthrough-major-implications-bitcoin/)

#### Bitcoin Optech Newsletter #432 recap podcast

Gustavo Flores Echaiz and Mike Schmidt are joined by jungly to discuss a framework for decentralized mining pool payouts and the disclosure of a DoS vulnerability in Eclair. [bitcoinops.org](https://bitcoinops.org/en/podcast/2026/11/24/)
