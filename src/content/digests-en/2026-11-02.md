## Security & Privacy

#### BTCPay Server urges shutdowns after reported hot wallet drains on older versions

The team traced an attack vector to versions 2.4.1 and older, has no credible reports of drains on the latest 2.4.7 and urges users to update. Founder Nicolas Dorier said the attacker logged in as admin on affected instances; how is still under investigation. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/bitcoin-payment-processor-btcpay-hit-by-hot-wallet-theft-reports/) [x.com](https://x.com/BtcpayServer/status/2117020340815819262)

#### Preprint estimates 71% of an exposed LND node cohort remained on affected release lines

The October 21 preprint's estimate covers a cohort of exposed LND nodes as of its October 13 cutoff, not the whole network; CryptoSlate reports the missing security fixes are about a year old. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-lightnings-year-old-security-fixes-still-missing-from-71-of-flagged-nodes/)

## Technology & Development

#### waxwing updates Lichen L2 design, using BABE to cut on-chain dispute costs

Using BABE instead of BitVMX cuts a full dispute to about 4–5 transactions from 20–25 and on-chain cost to about 30 kvB from 200, though users must store 240MB. waxwing says it is implemented and is deploying a hub on mutinynet. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/eed59d04-8620-4b04-b97e-48d2432e74fbn@googlegroups.com/) [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/eed59d04-8620-4b04-b97e-48d2432e74fbn@googlegroups.com/1.1-a.txt)

#### 0xB10C suspects unsolicited rune-mint transaction chains degraded compact block reconstruction

In his current understanding, someone pushed chains of ~100vB P2A rune-mint transactions to nodes unsolicited while another party burned the outputs, causing mempool differences. The activity stopped on Oct 18, and reconstruction rates then improved. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/stats-on-compact-block-reconstructions/1052?page=4#post_63) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/u/m4ycon)

#### dni machine-checks soundness of 128-bit BIP340 batch-verification randomizers in Lean

Proposals to cut the randomizers from 256 to 128 bits would speed libsecp256k1 batch verification by up to about 9%. The Lean 4 proof covers both deterministic derivations, leaving offline grinding: about 2^64 hashes for a roughly 2^-64 chance of an invalid batch passing. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/machine-checked-soundness-of-bip340-batch-verification-with-128-bit-derived-randomizers/3019) [github.com](https://github.com/sipa/bips/issues/219)

## Mining

#### Bitcoin mining revenue hits 11-month high despite steep hashprice decline

Miners closed October with their highest monthly revenue since November 2025, but the bitcoin price drop after the $6.3 billion Binance hack has weighed heavily on mining profitability. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/bitcoin-mining-revenue-hits-11-month-high-despite-brutal-hashprice-decline/)

#### AI wants Bitcoin miners' power, but the contracts show who really gets paid

A CryptoSlate analysis: miners spent years securing cheap electricity, substations and grid connections, and AI companies now want that access, turning aging mines into valuable real estate. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/ai-wants-bitcoin-miners-power-but-the-contracts-reveal-who-really-gets-paid/)

#### $1.4B in bitcoin from Binance hack, attributed by FBI to North Korea, remains untouched

About 18,270.2 BTC worth $1.4 billion was stolen in the Oct. 29 hack; the FBI identified North Korea as the principal perpetrator. The stolen coins remain dispersed and unmoved.
