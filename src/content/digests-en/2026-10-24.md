## Lightning & L2

#### Lightning Labs' Wavelength goes live on Bitcoin mainnet

An Ark implementation, the Wavelength SDK lets humans and agents send and receive bitcoin with a few API calls, self-custodial from the first command, with no node or liquidity to manage. Version v0.2.0 is out. [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2113637153070542859) [lightning.engineering](https://lightning.engineering/posts/2026-10-23-wavelength-mainnet-launch/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Researcher Nishant Bansal discloses a DoS flaw that crashes Core Lightning nodes

Versions v25.02 through v26.04 abort on a channel\_announcement naming the same node twice and crash again on every restart until the record is removed by hand. The attack needs only an attacker-controlled 2-of-2 output and on-chain fees. Upgrade to v26.04.1 or later. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/cln-self-referential-channel-announcement-dos-disclosure/2974#post_1) [nishantbansal2003.github.io](https://nishantbansal2003.github.io/posts/CLN-Self-Referential-Channel-Announcement-DoS/)

## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin Optech Newsletter #428 covers a single-issuer layer 2 with unilateral onchain exit

The issue describes a design whose users can redeem their bitcoin onchain without the issuer's cooperation and summarizes a proposal to include hash preimages in output script descriptors, plus its regular sections. [bitcoinops.org](https://bitcoinops.org/en/newsletters/2026/10/23/)

#### @adam3us makes the case for Simplicity as Bitcoin's last soft fork

The pitch on stage at Lugano Plan B: one expressive language with formal semantics could raise the security bar for Bitcoin scripts and open the door to opt-in Confidential Transactions or covenants. [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2113566802479624441)
