## Security & Privacy

#### Bitcoin Core fixes slow unbounded memory growth from paused pre-BIP31 peers

Rated medium severity: a malicious peer could pretend not to support BIP31 pings to avoid disconnection and grow its send queue by up to 2MB/hour (inbound) or 5MB/hour (outbound). Fixed in 29.5, 30.4, 31.2 and 32.1; -blocksonly nodes are much less affected. [bitcoincore.org](https://bitcoincore.org/en/2026/10/28/disclose-fpausesend-oom/) [github.com](https://github.com/project-loupe/loupe)

#### Liquid Federation plans to transfer remaining 600 BTC in coming days to restore full LBTC backing

The Federation says a 2.5 BTC test transaction succeeded. BTC-to-LBTC swaps have resumed via Aqua, Bull Bitcoin and SideSwap; peg-outs remain under enhanced security controls as an independent security audit nears completion. [x.com](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2115479634976411873) [x.com](https://x.com/AquaBitcoin/status/2115493479594045591)

The Fed held its target range at 3.75%–4% on Oct. 28 in a 10-2 vote; Lisa Cook and Beth Hammack dissented in favor of an immediate quarter-point hike.
