## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Twenty One Capital posts first profitable quarter with $1.09B net income on bitcoin gains

The Tether-backed company reported $1.087 billion in Q3 net income, driven largely by unrealized gains on its bitcoin holdings. Its shares slid despite the profit. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/twenty-one-capital-bitcoin-gains-first-profitable-quarter-1-09b/)

#### Blockstream introduces enterprise custody solution developed with Thales

Its Secure Multi-Party Computation (Secure-MPC) signing protocol runs entirely inside Thales hardware security modules, so key material stays protected even during signing. [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2121309584430125316) [blog.blockstream.com](https://blog.blockstream.com/press-releases/blockstream-launches-enterprise-custody-solution-developed-in-collaboration-with-thales)

## Security & Privacy

#### Blockstream test: today's hardware wallets can make post-quantum signatures

Per Blockstream, @n1ckler tested hash-based SHRINCS signatures on devices including Blockstream Jade. Signing took a few seconds to a minute and a half, so in principle no new hardware is needed. [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2121293645559074960)

#### Revised draft BIP proposes post-quantum address format using ML-DSA-65

Draft v0.2 moves to witness version 3 (bc1r addresses, as version 2 is used by BIP-360), adopts a BIP-341-based sighash, adds a key-type byte for future schemes and withdraws the SNARK aggregation prototype. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bip-draft-post-quantum-address-format-bc1z-using-ml-dsa-65/3067#post_2) [github.com](https://github.com/Zaenus/btc-safe/blob/4e09003f69ee276f8df1eadcc913ab98a84dbf52/docs/bip/0001-pq-address-format.md)

## Technology & Development

#### Demo STARK proof of Bitcoin's UTXO set at block 972,500, with circuits proven in Lean

A 1.6 MB proof, verifiable in a browser in about half a second, checks headers from genesis and replays blocks 935,001–972,500. It does not cover scripts or signatures, so trust matches assumevalid. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/a-stark-proof-of-bitcoins-utxo-set-at-block-972-500-with-circuits-proven-in-lean/3070) [starknet-innovation.github.io](https://starknet-innovation.github.io/starked-bitcoin/)

#### BIP proposal would add post-quantum ML-KEM key establishment to Bitcoin's P2P protocol

Liam Gilligan proposes ML-KEM-768 so that P2P traffic recorded today could not be decrypted by a future quantum attacker. Keys would derive from both the BIP-324 and ML-KEM secrets, keeping connections at least as secure as today. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/12a1a76d-0e27-4035-9fe8-a47524729c47n@googlegroups.com/) [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/12a1a76d-0e27-4035-9fe8-a47524729c47n@googlegroups.com/1.1-a.txt)

#### Bitcoin Optech #431 covers three vulnerabilities disclosed in older Bitcoin Core versions

The issue also links draft BIPs for exfiltration-resistant signing, announces a plan to mine the testnet5 genesis block and describes an investigation into degraded compact block reconstruction. [bitcoinops.org](https://bitcoinops.org/en/newsletters/2026/11/13/)
