## Price & Market

#### Bitcoin sinks to about $61,300, its lowest level since early July, as Asian stocks tumble

A global equity selloff sparked by the Anthropic IPO’s fading debut spread into Asia and forced South Korea’s exchange to halt program selling. BTC first slipped below $62,000 on Thursday. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/market-updates/bitcoin-price-lowest-since-july-asian-stocks-crash-kospi/)

## Funds & ETFs

#### BlackRock’s IBIT posts a record $541M daily outflow

It was IBIT’s largest daily outflow on record, bringing three-session outflows to nearly $900 million as BTC dipped amid a broader market selloff following Anthropic’s market debut. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/markets/blackrock-ibit-record-single-day-outflow)

#### Institutional holdings in US spot bitcoin ETFs reach a record, Galaxy Research says

According to Galaxy Research, 13F filers added 49,229 BTC through US spot bitcoin ETPs in Q3, lifting institutional holdings to a record 356,491 BTC, with Wells Fargo becoming the largest holder. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/institutional-bitcoin-etf-holdings)

## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Saylor says Strategy could shrink STRC to $5B and even sell bitcoin to defend it

Michael Saylor told investors that Strategy would rather shrink its flagship STRC preferred toward $5 billion than raise its 12% dividend, and that it could sell bitcoin to buy back the paper below par. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/saylor-strategy-shrink-strc-5-billion-sell-bitcoin/)

#### Sweden’s H100 could tap its bitcoin trading pool to fund share buybacks

Shareholders will vote on Dec. 7 on whether H100 can buy back up to 10% of its shares, potentially using a bitcoin pool that lost about 30 BTC in Q3. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/swedens-h100-13m-q3-loss-share-buyback-approval)

## Regulation & Policy

#### CLARITY Act likely dead until 2027, top lawmakers warn

House Financial Services Chairman French Hill said the crypto market structure bill’s chances in the lame-duck session are “not high”, pushing it toward a Democratic-led Congress. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/french-hill-clarity-act-unlikely-before)

## Technology & Development

#### SHRINCS researchers publish a draft security analysis of the proposed Bitcoin post-quantum signature scheme

The analysis is a draft and independent review is still needed. Open concerns include wallet safety, transaction costs and implementation complexity; Dan Boneh, Charles Guillemet and Yehuda Lindell have flagged implementation-level tradeoffs. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/technology/bitcoin-quantum-defense-change-value-proposition-btc-researcher/) [x.com](https://x.com/MARAFoundation_/status/2123447558747951461)

## Security & Privacy

#### BitBox Cresta update fixes several firmware issues and one BitBoxApp issue

BitBox says it has seen no sign of exploitation and urges all users to update; BitBoxApp v4.53.0 bundles firmware v9.29.0 and includes a bootloader update. The release also adds German firmware, optional in-app passphrase entry and Lightning contacts. [blog.bitbox.swiss](https://blog.bitbox.swiss/en/bitbox-11-2026-cresta-update/) [bitbox.swiss](https://bitbox.swiss/download?source=update)

#### Bisq v1.10.12 patches a serious Java vulnerability, team urges immediate update

Bisq says users should update ASAP even if they aren’t trading. The release fixes the Java4Shell flaw, includes a Tor security update and adds USD stablecoins and custom payment methods. [x.com](https://x.com/bisq_network/status/2123618478305530297) [github.com](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq/releases/tag/v1.10.12)

#### LNbits advisory details a webhook SSRF that bypasses its callback allowlist

According to the advisory, an unauthenticated caller can make an LNbits server send webhooks to internal addresses and use the 200/404 status to map internal ports, HTTP paths and cloud metadata such as IAM role names. [github.com](https://github.com/lnbits/lnbits/security/advisories/GHSA-qjqm-577g-5q7x)

## Lightning & L2

#### Amboss publishes three machine learning papers on Lightning routing and liquidity

Amboss says two of them already run in production and the work has allocated over $16 million of liquidity across managed nodes. The papers address liquidity placement, probing hidden balances and estimating channel splits from public data. [x.com](https://x.com/ambosstech/status/2123491521337167924) [amboss.tech](https://amboss.tech/blog/amboss-research-in-production)
