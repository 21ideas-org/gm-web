## Security & Privacy

#### Ledger confirms a hidden implant stole seed phrases from some devices sold via CryptoBilis

The implant read recovery words off the screen at setup; devices could pass Ledger's genuine check. Ledger ties all confirmed losses to CryptoBilis; buyers who set one up should move funds to a new device and seed. MediaMarkt Saturn halted Ledger sales after a German buyer reported an implant. [blog.casa.io](https://blog.casa.io/ledger-cryptobilis-implant-supply-chain-attack/) [docs.casa.io](https://docs.casa.io/wealth-security-protocol/chosen-features/heterogeneous-hardware-and-software)

#### Liquid Network announces final stages of recovery after 4,000-bitcoin hack

@Liquid\_BTC says user swaps are back via semi-automated swaps for Federation members; normal peg and balance validation will return soon. @AquaBitcoin reports 1:1 peg-ins and peg-outs via @side\_swap, while Lightning-to-Liquid swaps are still recovering. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/liquid-network-announces-recovery) [x.com](https://x.com/Liquid_BTC/status/2112526531096453606)

#### Eclair v0.14.5 brings security hardening fixes

ACINQ highly recommends upgrading, as some of the fixed issues can be exploited by malicious nodes. The patch release is fully compatible with earlier versions: stop Eclair, upgrade and restart, with no need to close channels. [github.com](https://github.com/ACINQ/eclair/releases/tag/v0.14.5)

#### Thieves hijack Ngrave alerts and drain roughly $2.56M

A fake security warning delivered through a trusted notification system sent users to the thieves; the hardware itself was not cracked. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/crypto-thieves-hijack-ngrave-alerts-and-walk-away-with-2-56m/)

## Lightning & L2

#### Lightning Labs paper details Layertree, a design where millions could share one Bitcoin output

According to @LightningNewsX, CTO Olaoluwa Osuntokun's 103-page paper tracks funds in an off-chain Merkle-sum tree; in the design, anyone can exit alone in one transaction and the operator cannot block it. It needs Bitcoin opcodes that are proposed but not yet activated. [x.com](https://x.com/LightningNewsX/status/2111032506212352250) [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2112613524065153120)
