## Price & Market

#### Bitcoin holds near $76,000 as Nasdaq slides into correction

## Mining

#### Core Scientific (CORZ) to exit bitcoin mining by year-end as colocation hits $215 million

The company will finish winding down mining in the fourth quarter and enter 2027 as a pure-play AI colocation landlord; its self-mining revenue has already fallen to $12.3 million. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/core-scientific-exits-bitcoin-year-end)

#### Democrats push to restrict new data centers, threatening bitcoin miners' AI pivot

After data centers were on the ballot this week, newly empowered Democrats from Texas to Ohio are talking about pausing or limiting new ones, a risk for miners betting their future on AI hosting. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/democrats-data-center-moratorium-bitcoin-miners/)

## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Keel (KEEL) sells entire bitcoin treasury for $192 million, will sell BTC daily

Keel sold 2,520 BTC in the third quarter, leaving its treasury near zero, and said its Quebec-mined coins will now be sold every day as it pivots to AI data centers. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/keel-sells-entire-bitcoin-treasury-192m)

## Regulation & Policy

#### Greece approves 10% capital gains tax on crypto, below the draft rate

Parliament adopted the country's first dedicated crypto tax framework, exempting annual gains of up to €500. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/greece-approves-10-crypto-gains-tax)

#### South Korea moves to delay 22% crypto gains tax to 2029 in a fourth postponement

The government will ask lawmakers to approve the delay and to let investors carry losses forward for up to three years. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/south-korea-crypto-tax-2029)

#### Fed and OCC quiz US banks after $6.3 billion Binance hack

Federal Reserve and OCC supervisors contacted US banks this week to check for vulnerabilities following the hack. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/fed-occ-quiz-banks-binance-hack)

#### Thailand files criminal complaint against Bitget, plans to block access in December

Thailand's securities regulator alleges that the exchange's operator ran an unlicensed digital asset business in the country. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/thailand-targets-bitget-criminal-complaint-imminent-ban/)

#### LearnBitcoin shares mainnet timestamp data for Great Consensus Cleanup grace-period debate

LearnBitcoin found that across 483 retargets, a period's first block was stamped before the previous period's last block 10 times; the worst was -1,127 s in 2012, -43 s since 2015, and nothing came near 7,200 s. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/great-consensus-cleanup-revival/710?page=5#post_95) [learnbitcoin.com](https://www.learnbitcoin.com/downloads/time-warp-seams.csv)
