## Lightning & L2

#### SideSwap reopens Bitcoin swaps while Liquid reserves stay locked

SideSwap funds the swaps from its own wallet; partly manual refills can delay payments, and direct Federation withdrawals remain restricted. Reserves are still 602 BTC short, according to @BitcoinArchive. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/sideswap-reopens-bitcoin-swaps-while-liquid-reserves-stay-locked/) [x.com](https://x.com/BitcoinArchive/status/2112956331699437820)

#### Soap adds Lightning payments via Voltage

According to @voltage\_cloud, merchants can now offer Lightning in Soap's checkout while Voltage handles the infrastructure behind it. [x.com](https://x.com/voltage_cloud/status/2112904545810485356) [voltage.cloud](https://voltage.cloud/blog/soap-adds-lightning-payments-through-voltage)

#### andrewclicks presents Electrum plugins to ease Lightning onboarding for merchants

A CLINK plugin routes payment requests via nostr relays so an Electrum wallet can receive unsolicited payments without opening ports; a liquidity plugin automates submarine swaps to maintain inbound liquidity. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/several-projects-to-make-merchant-adoption-easier-and-cheaper/2967) [github.com](https://github.com/BareBits/electrum_clink)

## Technology & Development

#### 0xB10C doubts addr relay can reliably coordinate TCP hole punching between home nodes

In a simulator, 0xB10C saw unreachable nodes receive address self-announcements far less reliably than listening nodes, as they have only 8 connections, so an option relying on both home nodes receiving them would probably be very unreliable. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/tcp-hole-punching-for-bitcoin-nodes-behind-home-nats/2497?page=2#post_29) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/tcp-hole-punching-for-bitcoin-nodes-behind-home-nats/2497/22)

#### Pieter Wuille adds Lean-proven chain-length bound for BIP-54 timewarp fixes

The updated post includes an accurate formula for the w-bound, now proven in linked Lean code along with its decimal approximation. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bounds-on-chain-length-with-bip-54-timewarp-fixes/2899?page=2#post_29)
