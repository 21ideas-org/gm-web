## Security & Privacy

#### LND update blocks one exploit route, but a critical Linux flaw remains

LND's October 30 releases block a demonstrated MPTCP exploit route; affected hosts still need a patched Linux kernel and a reboot. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-lightnings-security-update-shuts-one-door-a-critical-linux-flaw-remains/)

#### @nvk: at least 15 external reviews touched COLDCARD before its seed-generation defect was found

According to @nvk, these included paid reviews of the Mk4 (2022) and Q (2024); a pre-incident AI-assisted review by Rob Ham yielded 85 candidate findings but missed the seed-generation defect, which he says a new AI model found. [x.com](https://x.com/nvk/status/2119819982770954324) [x.com](https://x.com/Coinkite/status/2119874817151402030)

#### Passport Prime Legacy Mode 1.1.0 adds passphrase support and fixes a key-export privacy issue

The fix stops connected software from using one Legacy app to export another coin's public keys (e.g. Bitcoin keys via the Ethereum app) without on-device confirmation. Legacy Mode, Ethereum and Solana apps must all be updated to 1.1.0 together. [foundation.xyz](https://foundation.xyz/blog/legacy-mode-v1-1-0-is-now-available) [github.com](https://github.com/Foundation-Devices/Legacy-Apps/releases/tag/v1.1.0)

## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Strategy buys 1,036 BTC, lifting holdings to 850,364 BTC

According to its latest SEC filing, Strategy spent $81.9 million on bitcoin last week and $188.3 million repurchasing STRC preferred shares. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/strategy-buy-1036-bitcoin-holdings-850364-btc) [x.com](https://x.com/JAN3Financial/status/2119783794299281502)

#### Amundi raises Strategy (MSTR) stake 44% to $291 million

Europe's largest asset manager held 1.9 million shares at the end of the third quarter, a 13F shows; MSTR has since fallen about 18% from quarter-end. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/amundi-boosts-strategy-mstr-stake-44)

#### Strive (ASST) adds 29 BTC for $2.2 million, lifting holdings to 43,153 BTC

It bought from Oct. 31 to Nov. 6 at an average of $75,721 as weekly purchases slowed; cash slipped to $487.6 million. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/strive-asst-adds-29-bitcoin-holdings-43153)

## Regulation & Policy

#### Coinbase custody arm wins full federal trust charter from the OCC

The charter upgrades April's conditional approval, but does not let it take deposits, make loans or issue stablecoins. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/coinbase-full-occ-national-trust-charter)

#### Binance to close accounts in France, Spain, Italy and Poland on Dec. 31

Binance told users to withdraw their funds; deposits are blocked and a monthly fee on idle balances starts Nov. 29. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/binance-closing-accounts-four-eu-countries)
