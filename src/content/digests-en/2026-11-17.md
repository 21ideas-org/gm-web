## Security & Privacy

#### Breach at Trezor shipping partner Skladon exposes data from about 27,000 orders

Trezor believes names, phone numbers, addresses, emails and order details from orders placed Aug. 9–Nov. 7 were exported. It says its own systems and devices weren't compromised, and US customers aren't affected. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/hackers-strike-trezor-shipping-partner-exposing-wallet-buyers-personal-details/) [x.com](https://x.com/Trezor/status/2122363569891135655)

#### Hackers hit Ledger and 177 other websites with crypto address-swapping code via Optimizely

Attackers hijacked the web testing platform Optimizely to plant crypto-stealing, address-swapping code on the websites of 178 organizations, including hardware wallet maker Ledger. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/optimizely-hack-ledger-178-brands-crypto-address-swap/) [x.com](https://x.com/DonjonLedger/status/2122596246233698306)

## Regulation & Policy

#### German Bundestag approves bill that would let customs freeze crypto over €100,000 without a criminal case

The measure would apply to crypto and other assets of unclear origin worth more than €100,000. The bill now goes to the Bundesrat. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/german-bundestag-crypto-over-100000)

#### Supreme Court ends Custodia Bank's fight for a Fed master account

By denying Custodia's petition in No. 26-62, the Court left in place a Tenth Circuit ruling that Federal Reserve Banks may reject even eligible applicants, leaving the Fed free to deny crypto banks access. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/supreme-court-ends-custodia-fed-master)

## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Strategy buys $33M in bitcoin and spends $228M on STRC buybacks

Strategy added 485 BTC for $33.4M and repurchased $228.1M of STRC preferred shares, largely funded by new common stock sales. A CryptoSlate analysis finds it spent about $1.4B buying STRC back near $100, despite a stated plan to slow purchases there. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/strategy-buy-485-bitcoin-228-million-strc-buyback) [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/strategy-planned-to-slow-strc-buybacks-near-100-but-spent-another-1-4-billion-instead/)

#### Strive buys 251 BTC for $17.5M, partly funded by preferred stock sales

The purchase expands one of the largest corporate BTC treasuries and was partly backed by fresh preferred-stock funding. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/strive-uses-preferred-stock-sales-fund-17-5m-bitcoin-purchase/)

#### OranjeBTC proposes preferred shares as its stock falls 37% in 2026
