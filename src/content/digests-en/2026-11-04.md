## Security & Privacy

#### Binance says a fake job interview and compromised AI tools led to its $6.3 billion hack

Binance disclosed that hackers used fake job interviews, compromised developer tools and internal AI systems to infiltrate the exchange, stealing about $6.34 billion in crypto last week. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/binance-fake-job-interview-compromised-ai-tools-6-3b-hack/)

#### LDK fix blocks a probe linking private Lightning channels to their onchain funding

LDK's October 25 fix rejects a funding-location lookup used by the probe, which requires connecting to both endpoints of an unannounced channel. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-lightning-fix-blocks-a-probe-linking-private-channels-to-onchain-funding/)

## Regulation & Policy

#### Moscow Exchange plans to launch bitcoin, ether and USDT trading in December

Trading is planned to start on Dec. 1 under Russia's new law, with annual purchase limits for non-qualified investors. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/moscow-exchange-bitcoin-ether-usdt-trading-december)

#### TD Cowen: a Democratic Congress would sink the CLARITY Act for two years

TD Cowen's Jaret Seiberg says a Democratic sweep of Congress would also hand Elizabeth Warren the Banking Committee and let Democrats block Trump's picks for the SEC and CFTC. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/td-cowen-democratic-congress-clarity-act)

#### South Korean regulators call for tighter cold-wallet controls at crypto firms

Regulators urged more cold-wallet storage and tighter internal access controls days after hackers took $6.3 billion in digital assets from Binance. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/south-korean-regulator-demands-tighter-cold-wallet-controls-crypto-firms/)
