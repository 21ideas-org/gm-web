## Security & Privacy

#### LNbits tells users to stop their servers immediately, citing a wave of attacks

LNbits says security patches are coming. It disabled all instances on its SaaS, saying services would resume within 24 hours, and told operators to disable the Paywall extension while it investigates a potential bug. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/lightning-network-security-scare-forces-lnbits-servers-offline/) [x.com](https://x.com/lnbits/status/2119428804951048237)

#### Memory-exhaustion DoS disclosed in Eclair 0.14.0, fixed in 0.14.1

Any peer able to connect to an Eclair 0.14.0 node, even without a channel, could make it buffer an unlimited number of commitment\_signed messages until it runs out of memory. Users should upgrade to 0.14.1 or later. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/disclosure-memory-exhaustion-dos-in-eclair-0-14-0/3057) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-9002-memory-exhaustion-dos-via-start_batch-in-eclair-0140-1)

## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin block packs 11,255 transactions, the second-highest on record

The Nov. 7 block came within reach of a transaction record set in 2015, in a year already producing extraordinary daily transaction counts. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/bitcoin-packs-11255-transactions-into-a-single-block-nears-record/)

#### Draft v2.1 of a co-signer spending policies BIP published

It adds strict verifier semantics, versioned commitments, signed finality and a k-of-n fix. The full schema, exact-byte vectors and reference implementation remain unfinished; the author is seeking feedback, including on serialization. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bip-draft-co-signer-spending-policies/2955#post_6) [groups.google.com](https://groups.google.com/g/bitcoindev/c/yoL5VVMO4Qg)
