## Technology & Development

#### Fulcrum 2.1.4 speeds up some history lookups by up to 5x–10x

Maintenance release: Storage::getHistory, behind subscriptions and history RPCs, is 5x–10x faster for uncached lookups, such as after a cold start. A new history\_lookup\_threads setting controls it; the default suits almost all systems. [github.com](https://github.com/cculianu/Fulcrum/releases/tag/v2.1.4)

#### Adam Gibson explores variable payout amounts for BABE, as applied to Lichen

BABE's approach targets witness encryption for Groth16 proof verification, which would let a coin on bitcoin be unlocked only by someone holding a valid SNARK proof. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/variable-payout-amounts-for-babe-as-applied-to-lichen/2963#post_1) [eprint.iacr.org](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/065)

#### MyNode v0.3.50 released with LND v0.21.4 and optional automatic upgrades
