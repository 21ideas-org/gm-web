## Institutions & Treasuries

#### MSCI's new index rules could squeeze Strategy's bitcoin buying

MSCI will exclude companies classified as non-operating businesses from its Global Investable Market Indexes; Strategy shares fell 9.6% in the first full session after the Oct. 15 announcement, though its 848,250 BTC remain about $5 billion above cost. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/strategys-mstr-69-decline-exposes-a-bigger-danger-behind-mscis-2-8-billion-selling-threat/) [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/market-updates/strategy-mstr-falls-msci-ruling-saylor-bitcoin-5b-profit/)

#### Strategy plans daily STRC dividends from Nov. 2, pending shareholder approval

The plan requires shareholders to approve changes to the dividend structure; Strategy intends to keep STRC's 12% annual dividend rate. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/bitcoin-giant-strategy-strc-daily-dividends-starting-november/)

#### Deutsche Bank nears launch of institutional bitcoin and ether custody

The bank ties the move to a target of 8% compound annual growth in its corporate banking services by 2028; co-head Gerald Podobnik said the services will complement its traditional offerings. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/deutsche-bank-nears-launch-institutional-bitcoin-ether-custody/)

#### Capital B reports $78M H1 loss driven by a bitcoin impairment charge

Europe's second-largest bitcoin treasury raised $30 million and grew its bitcoin holdings by 11% in the first half of 2026, despite a $75 million bitcoin impairment loss. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/capital-b-78m-h1-loss-bitcoin-impairment)
