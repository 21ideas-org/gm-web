## Lightning & L2

#### pi402 lets coding agents pay for AI inference over Lightning, with no accounts or API keys

The fully open-source L402+MPP stack is built on Pi and Aperture; its companion daemon, lightmeter, handles per-token metering in sats and model pricing. [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2120977508724089012) [pi402.tech](https://pi402.tech/)

#### Tony proposes a K-of-N custody design for Lightning nodes

The proposed architecture combines threshold ECDSA signing with threshold encryption of offline-precomputed revocation secrets, aiming to stop any single online machine from compromising channel funds without a spec change. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/towards-a-k-of-n-lightning-network-node/2395#post_14) [github.com](https://github.com/spiralxyz/bitcoin-payments)

#### Amboss reports 3–6% more efficient Lightning balance probing with Bayesian Binary Search

Instead of splitting at the midpoint, the method splits a channel's balance range where the balance is likely to sit. The paper, with Stillmark, appeared in the peer-reviewed journal Algorithms in 2025; the method runs in production at Amboss. [x.com](https://x.com/ambosstech/status/2120954804394676342) [amboss.tech](https://amboss.tech/blog/bayesian-binary-search)
