## Technology & Development

#### btc-verified October update: anchoring a formal model of Bitcoin consensus to the real network

The project has built a minimal formal model of Bitcoin consensus; this month ProofOfKeags tries to anchor it to the live network, since directly verifying Bitcoin Core's modern C++ is far out of reach with current resources. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/btc-verified-october-update-connecting-map-to-territory/2964) [proofofkeags.com](https://proofofkeags.com/research/2026-10-19-connecting-map-to-territory.html)

#### Opt-in exposure-aware transaction construction for wallets proposed on bitcoindev

A companion to bitcoin/bips PR #2294, the proposed policy would have a wallet also spend outputs whose public keys a spend would reveal and send change to a fresh, non-exposed condition, preserving resistance to BIP 360 long exposure attacks. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/CAAa3hFNACEoE4KXi06SaqGM0qsOTrgT3HB6X2oiocp--r6-rmg@mail.gmail.com/) [github.com](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0360.mediawiki)
