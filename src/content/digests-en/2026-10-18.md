## Security & Privacy

#### Liquid peg restoration still underway, according to @Excellion

@Excellion says an external security audit and Blockstream's internal coordination on how to fund a 600 BTC shortfall are running in parallel, and that Indra swaps for payments continue, with limits rising to 250k sats per swap. [x.com](https://x.com/Excellion/status/2111403013797482679) [x.com](https://x.com/AquaBitcoin/status/2111403661855277107)

#### Lopp's wrench attack map reaches 702 incidents after AI-assisted search

Researcher Pizza Bitcoiner used AI-assisted searches to uncover hundreds of overlooked cases, more than doubling Jameson Lopp's archive of physical attacks on crypto holders. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/lopps-wrench-attack-map-hits-702-after-ai-assisted-search-work/)

#### Eclair patches a browser attack that could send commands to Lightning nodes

The Oct. 15 release blocks a cached-authentication route to Eclair's API and requires browser interfaces that connect to it directly to add a backend. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/eclair-blocks-browser-requests-that-could-let-a-webpage-control-a-bitcoin-lightning-node/)

## Regulation & Policy

#### French National Assembly narrowly adopts stablecoin swap tax proposal

If passed, the measure would subject French holders to a 31.4% capital gains tax each time they swap a cryptocurrency for a stablecoin; analysts say it would push holders to look for other places to park their assets. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/french-assembly-narrowly-adopts-stablecoin-swap-tax-proposal/)

#### EU joins eight nations in signing Warsaw Protocol on crypto crime

The treaty modernizes the Warsaw Convention, adding crypto assets to its scope and expanding the powers of financial intelligence units over them, with an emphasis on international cooperation and faster information requests. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/eu-joins-8-nations-to-sign-warsaw-protocol-on-crypto-crime/)
