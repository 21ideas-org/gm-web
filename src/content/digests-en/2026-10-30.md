## Price & Market

#### Bitcoin briefly slides to $76,300 as Binance confirms a hack estimated at $6.3 billion

Binance's initial findings point to a possible employee laptop compromise, with no evidence of a private key leak. About $3 billion has been frozen onchain and Binance pledged to cover all losses. TRM Labs and ZachXBT say North Korea's Lazarus Group likely carried it out. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/bitcoin-price-slides-near-76000-binance) [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/binance-releases-first-6-3b-hack-findings-reports-no-key-leak-evidence/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Bitcoin developers fix three security flaws uncovered by AI

An October 28 advisory identifies four patched releases; the 29.x series has now reached end of life. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-developers-rush-to-fix-3-security-flaws-after-ai-uncovers-them/) [bitcoincore.org](https://bitcoincore.org/en/2026/10/28/disclose-undo-overflow/)

#### BTCPay Server 2.4.7 fixes 85 security findings from Anthropic's OSS Scanner

By the scan report's original ratings: 1 critical, 13 high, 52 medium and 19 low. The bug-fix release also addresses reliability and payment-accounting issues; updating is highly recommended, especially for servers shared by multiple merchants. [github.com](https://github.com/btcpayserver/btcpayserver/releases/tag/v2.4.7)

#### Trezor warns CryptoBilis buyers after a reported hardware implant in a Trezor Safe 5

Trezor hasn't received the device yet but treats the report as credible; no buyer has reported losses. Anyone who bought a Trezor from CryptoBilis since March 2026 should move funds to a wallet with a new seed and not set up unused devices. [x.com](https://x.com/Trezor/status/2116062963752747040) [trezor.io](https://trezor.io/support#open-chat)

#### Tampered Ledgers were reported in Singapore a month before the news broke

Singapore police say tampered Ledger devices started showing up there on September 10. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/tampered-ledger-reported-in-singapore-a-month-before-news-broke-asia-express)

## Technology & Development

#### Blockstream researchers say they cut post-quantum signing on a hardware wallet to about 3 seconds

According to Blockstream, signing previously took over 20 seconds, and the method works on hardware users already have. [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2115909357238206836) [blog.blockstream.com](https://blog.blockstream.com/hardware-wallets-post-quantum-signatures/)

#### Laz1m0v releases a 371-byte Tapscript multiplication core that needs no soft fork

OP\_MUL has been disabled since 2010. The witness-assisted construction multiplies values up to 46,340 within the 4-byte number limit and comes with 375- and 377-byte division extensions, a Rocq proof and a regtest harness. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/multiplication-in-tapscript-today-371-bytes-no-soft-fork/3010) [github.com](https://github.com/Laz1mov/tapscript-mul16-unrolled)

#### Sparrow Wallet 2.5.6 improves accessibility and updates its bundled Tor

The release upgrades Java to v25.0.5, removing eMudhra root certificates, and bundled Tor to v0.4.9.5 to meet newly required client protocols, and improves screen reader and keyboard access. [github.com](https://github.com/sparrowwallet/sparrow/releases/tag/2.5.6)
