## Regulation & Policy

#### Russia's VTB opens bitcoin trading for qualified investors

VTB began letting qualified investors trade bitcoin in its app on Friday, a day after the Bank of Russia approved the knowledge test every crypto buyer must pass. SPB Exchange plans to open spot trading on Dec. 1. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/russia-vtb-bitcoin-trading-crypto-test)

#### Spain lets its anti-money laundering unit freeze crypto accounts without a judge

A royal decree lets Sepblac, Spain's financial intelligence unit, freeze crypto, bank and payment accounts for up to five business days without a court order. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/spain-sepblac-freeze-crypto-accounts)

#### Japan weighs safeguards for bitcoin holders who keep their own keys

Japan's financial regulator is examining what exchanges can do to prevent scams and what could protect users after they withdraw their funds. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/japan-weighs-safeguards-for-bitcoin-holders-who-keep-their-own-keys/)

## Technology & Development

#### @conduition suggests an extensions field to commit to post-quantum witness data

The design adds a new transaction field for format extensions, initially for post-quantum signature schemes, and builds on @sipa's idea of chaining witness-style commitments: each extension commits to the next by recursive hashing. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/segwit-commitment-to-post-quantum-witness-data/2702#post_17) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/u/sipa)

#### StartOS v0.4.3-rc.4 release candidate adds shared volumes and UPS monitoring

On Server Pure, the first start after updating also updates firmware: the server is offline for up to 5 minutes and must not be unplugged or powered off. Also new: per-component temperature metrics and registry sync over WebSocket. [github.com](https://github.com/Start9Labs/start-technologies/releases/tag/start-os/v0.4.3-rc.4)

#### New Liana wallets now default to Taproot for more private recovery setups

In a SegWit multisig wallet, every spend reveals all primary and recovery keys and the timelock; with Taproot, an everyday spend shows only the primary keys and their signatures, keeping the recovery path hidden behind a hash. [x.com](https://x.com/ivygalindo/status/2125280873922560171) [x.com](https://x.com/lianabitcoin/status/2126362717887848602)
