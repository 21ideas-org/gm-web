## Regulation & Policy

#### US Treasury orders MEXC cut off from the US financial system over alleged $16 billion in illicit flows

FinCEN's final order under section 9714(a) will bar US financial institutions from sending or receiving funds with MEXC Global 30 days after its publication in the Federal Register. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/fincen-cuts-off-mexc-us-financial-system)

#### Rule that would allow crypto in 401(k)s reaches White House for final review

The Labor Department's final rule, which would create a fiduciary safe harbor for private equity, real estate and crypto in 401(k) menus, arrived at OIRA on Thursday, putting its public release within reach. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/dol-rule-crypto-401k-white-house-review)

#### US Treasury and Fed withdraw proposal to track international bitcoin and crypto transfers as small as $250

The existing $3,000 threshold stays in place as officials reassess the rules proposed in 2020. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/us-treasury-fed-abandon-plan-track-smaller-bitcoin-crypto-transfers/)

#### India's Enforcement Directorate searches CoinDCX over alleged routing of trades to Binance

The ED alleges that CoinDCX and Delta Exchange India routed users' trades to Binance, Bybit and other offshore platforms; its Bengaluru office searched three premises tied to Neblio Technologies. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/india-ed-searches-coindcx-delta-exchange-binance/)

## Security & Privacy

#### French crypto holder gets 30 months in prison for shooting at attackers during a kidnapping attempt

The shooting took place on Halloween in Vendée; the man had been targeted before, having already been kidnapped in 2022. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/french-crypto-holder-30-months-prison-shooting-attackers/)

#### Keystone asks Nexus App users to update to v1.3.7 after a cyberattack on its services

Keystone says its investigation so far found no evidence that user assets or personal data were affected. v1.3.7 adds security hardening; a later post said the iOS update is live. Check that the developer is listed as Yanssie HK Limited. [x.com](https://x.com/KeystoneWallet/status/2118664292736323953) [x.com](https://x.com/KeystoneWallet/status/2118921487034056827)

#### Connor Avila publishes draft BIPs for anti-exfil signing with BIP340 and ECDSA

Anti-exfil stops a compromised signing device from leaking its seed through nonces. The drafts add three PSBT input fields; the reference code has not been run on a device, reviewed by a cryptographer or audited. ## Technology & Development

#### Great Script Restoration proposal grows to five documents defining Tapleaf 0xc2

The proposed second Taproot leaf version would restore opcodes disabled in Bitcoin 0.3.1, add transaction introspection and extended primitives, and cap validation cost with a varops budget. Existing script types are unchanged. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/34b14b4f-7dce-466e-9d80-1af3b465c40fn@googlegroups.com/) [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/34b14b4f-7dce-466e-9d80-1af3b465c40fn@googlegroups.com/1.1-a.txt)

#### Bitcoin Optech Newsletter #430: TCP hole punching and a draft BIP for co-signer policies

The issue covers continued discussion of TCP hole punching to make nodes behind home routers reachable, and a draft BIP standardizing the spending policies co-signers enforce when emulating covenants. [bitcoinops.org](https://bitcoinops.org/en/newsletters/2026/11/06/)

#### Antoine Poinsot suggests the Stale Tip Relay proposal start by relaying only stale headers

He argues headers alone give most of the benefits without affecting mining incentives, while relaying full stale blocks would first require analysis of its effect on the incentive structure. #### Blockstream ships release 0.78.1 with memory wiping and stricter signing checks

Sensitive data is now wiped from memory after use and sighash flags are validated before signing with any signer. Singlesig's default bitcoin min fee rate drops to 0.1 sat/vB, and concurrent Electrum TLS connection failures are fixed. [github.com](https://github.com/Blockstream/gdk/releases/tag/release_0.78.1)
