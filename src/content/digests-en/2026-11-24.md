#### Bitcoin ETF outflows continue even as BlackRock’s IBIT shares gain nearly 5%

## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Strategy buys 457 BTC and spends $212M on preferred share buybacks

Strategy raised its STRC buyback authorization for the third time, to $4 billion, after using nearly $2.88 billion of the previous $3 billion limit. It also authorized moving up to $1 billion from its dollar reserve. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/strategy-buy-457-bitcoin-btc-treasury-losses) [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/strategy-raises-its-buyback-ceiling-to-4-billion-as-mstr-shares-sink-21/)

#### Strategy’s market value falls below the value of its bitcoin holdings

Strategy’s market value is near $35.7 billion, while the 851,305 BTC it held per its latest Form 8-K filing are worth roughly $51 billion. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/saylor-strategy-worth-less-than-bitcoin-holdings/)

#### Twenty One Capital buys $20 million of bitcoin after the price drops below $60,000

Two purchases lifted the company’s reported holdings to about 43,841 BTC and equaled nearly one-fifth of its cash and equivalents as of Sept. 30. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoins-plunge-below-60000-triggers-20-million-buying-spree-at-twenty-one-capital/)

#### Cross River Bank and LendingUSA launch bitcoin-backed loans of up to $250,000

Cross River Bank’s first bitcoin-backed loans let eligible borrowers access $10,000 to $250,000 without selling their bitcoin, at 30%, 50% or 70% loan-to-value. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/finance/lendingusa-cross-river-bank-bitcoin-backed-loans-250k/) [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/cross-river-bank-bitcoin-backed-loans)

## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin Core v31.3 released

The release brings bug fixes, performance improvements and updated translations. Notable changes include a net\_processing update to process only unique transaction INVs. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bitcoin-core-v31-3-released/3110) [bitcoincore.org](https://bitcoincore.org/bin/bitcoin-core-31.3/)

#### Draft BIP proposed for proving a transaction’s inclusion in a block by its wtxid

Jean Pablo’s draft documents the verifier checks behind differing formats in Knots, Sjors’s sketch, an Electrum protocol draft and an electrs PR, with a Python reference, 31 proofs that must verify and 62 that must be rejected. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/C899B11E-4971-4AAF-8BA3-81EBAFBF7739@jeanpablo.jp/) [github.com](https://github.com/jeanpablojp/bips/blob/witness-inclusion-proofs/bip-witness-inclusion-proofs.md)

#### Bitcoin’s UTXO set sheds 25 million entries as the blockchain keeps growing

Bitcoin averaged about 669,000 confirmed transactions a day in the week through Nov. 22, while its unspent-output count sat near its February 2024 level. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoins-blockchain-keeps-growing-as-its-live-ledger-sheds-25-million-entries/)
