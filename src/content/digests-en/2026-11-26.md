## Regulation & Policy

#### Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill clears House committee, heads to the floor

Rep. Nick Begich's bill would write the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve into law with a 20-year ban on selling its bitcoin; it is now eligible for a House floor vote. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill-house-floor)

#### Switzerland picks 57 countries for crypto tax data sharing starting in 2028

Switzerland approved 57 partner jurisdictions, including all EU states and the UK, for automatic exchanges of crypto tax data under the OECD's CARF from 2028. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/switzerland-57-countries-crypto-tax)

## Security & Privacy

#### Braiins warns of hashrate hijacking attacks exploiting Stratum V1

The mining pool operator says a series of attacks redirected up to 1.9 EH/s of hashrate by exploiting longstanding security weaknesses in the Stratum V1 mining protocol. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/braiins-warns-of-widespread-bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-attacks/)

#### @ismaelsadeeq's simulation shows Bitcoin Core wallet rebroadcast can link addresses to node IPs

A long-known leak: wallets re-announce unconfirmed transactions after 12-36h. In the simulation, an attacker paying an address linked it to the node tracking it 100% of the time; @mzumsande notes #36482 only slightly raises the attack's cost. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/exploiting-wallet-rebroadcast-to-link-addresses-to-node-ips-and-more/3123) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-9136-introduction-1)

## Technology & Development

#### Keith Mukai benchmarks post-quantum signatures on SeedSigner-class hardware

With Claude doing the work, he timed schemes on five boards; the set matching stateless SHRINCS signed in 1.93–7.71 s, 21–22% less time than SLH-DSA-SHA2-128s. Not checked against the draft's test vectors. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/shrincs-324-byte-stateful-post-quantum-signatures-with-static-backups/2158?page=2#post_24) [kdmukai-bot.github.io](https://kdmukai-bot.github.io/post-quantum-signature-benchmarks/)

#### Ledger Bitcoin app 2.6.0 reworks MuSig2 session IDs

Since 2.4.0, session IDs hashed the transaction, which blocked pre-generating nonces for future transactions. Version 2.6.0 bases them on the BIP-388 wallet policy plus optional PSBT entropy, letting wallets run parallel sessions. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/state-minimization-in-musig2-signing-sessions/626#post_11) [github.com](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/9f3d3bc6a60db16efbdc71ac8f7ea30217fc0c39/bip-0388.mediawiki)
