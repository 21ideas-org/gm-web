## Security & Privacy

#### Electrum 4.8.3 fixes flaw that let malicious servers trick users into paying attackers

A malicious server could hide a just-broadcast transaction and show an invalid conflicting one paying the attacker; fee-bumping it signed a real payment. Electrum reports attacks in the wild, with one user losing funds. Users of their own trusted servers are unaffected. [github.com](https://github.com/spesmilo/electrum/security/advisories/GHSA-6jp4-8v48-cc5v) [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/electrum-wallet-attack-hid-bitcoin-theft-behind-a-fee-increase/)

#### Keystone discloses website breach exposing customers' shipping data

Names, contact details, addresses and order details were exposed; Keystone says payment details, recovery phrases and private keys were not. It warns of scammers posing as Keystone and says it will never ask for a recovery phrase. [x.com](https://x.com/KeystoneWallet/status/2114054080754770137) [keyst.one](http://keyst.one/)
