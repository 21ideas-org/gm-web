## Price & Market

#### Bitcoin trades near $76,000 as Nasdaq slides into correction

Crypto liquidations topped $1 billion, while Strategy, Coinbase and bitcoin miners sold off by double digits. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/bitcoin-price-trades-near-76000-nasdaq)

## Mining

#### Core Scientific (CORZ) to exit bitcoin mining by year-end as colocation hits $215 million

The company will finish winding down mining in the fourth quarter and enter 2027 as a pure-play AI colocation landlord; self-mining revenue has already fallen to $12.3 million. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/core-scientific-exits-bitcoin-year-end)

#### Keel (KEEL) sells its bitcoin treasury for $192 million, will sell mined BTC daily

Keel sold 2,520 BTC in the third quarter, leaving its treasury near zero, and said Quebec-mined coins will now be sold every day as it pivots to AI data centers. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/keel-sells-entire-bitcoin-treasury-192m)

#### American Bitcoin (ABTC) posts about $126 million profit on $197 million holdings gain

Third-quarter net income was driven by a $197.3 million gain on its bitcoin holdings, reversing a $57.2 million loss in the prior quarter. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/american-bitcoin-126m-q3-profit)

## Regulation & Policy

#### Greece approves 10% capital gains tax on crypto

Parliament approved the country's first dedicated crypto tax framework at a rate below the draft; annual gains up to €500 are exempt. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/greece-approves-10-crypto-gains-tax)

#### South Korea proposes a fourth delay of its 22% crypto gains tax, to 2029

The government will ask lawmakers to approve the delay and to let investors carry losses forward for up to three years. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/south-korea-crypto-tax-2029)

#### National Bank of Ukraine opposes crypto regulation bill

The NBU declined to back the bill legalizing and regulating cryptocurrencies, which the Verkhovna Rada's finance committee approved for a second reading. [bits.media](https://bits.media/natsbank-ukrainy-vystupil-protiv-zakonoproekta-o-goskontrole-kriptovalyut/)

#### Thailand files criminal complaint against Bitget, plans December ban

The securities regulator alleges the exchange's operator ran an unlicensed digital asset business and plans to block access in the country in December. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/thailand-targets-bitget-criminal-complaint-imminent-ban/)
