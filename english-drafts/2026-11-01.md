## Funds & ETFs

#### Bitcoin ETFs lose $643M after Binance hack ends four-day inflow run

Friday's $643.33 million in net outflows wiped out four straight days of inflows and pushed the week into negative territory. Ether and solana funds also saw heavy redemptions as the hack fueled risk aversion. ## Security & Privacy

#### MyNode v0.3.52 fixes CVE-2026-98432 and adds other security mitigations

The update brings a kernel fix for PC platforms, LND v0.21.6 with an MPTCP mitigation, and disables MPTCP on Raspberry Pi. All users are advised to upgrade as soon as possible. [x.com](https://x.com/mynodebtc/status/2116363414079684842) [x.com](https://x.com/mynodebtc/status/2116540617887023370)

Start9 says the release contains important security updates. #### Antoine Riard argues BIP54's time-warp fix would not rule out forward blocks

On the bitcoindev list, Riard says a side "forward chain" still seems possible after BIP54 activation, and argues the forward block idea itself is cryptoeconomically unsound because transactions are processed twice. #### AaronZhang builds an eltoo channel on signet a third time, now with TEMPLATEHASH, CSFS and INTERNALKEY

After Inquisition 29.4 enabled OP\_TEMPLATEHASH, the test replaced a deliberately published old state with a newer one. The update leaf shrank to 10 B with a constant 143 B witness, versus 42 B and a growing 512 B witness with CSFS+CTV. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/eltoo-state-chain-on-signet-third-time-templatehash-csfs-internalkey/3012) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/eltoo-state-chain-on-signet-three-rounds-six-transactions-apo-ctv/2413)

#### RoboSats v0.8.8-alpha introduces garage keys and enables Nostr encrypted chat

The alpha release replaces the robot key with a garage key, embeds Tor in the Android app, verifies payouts against the Lightning node and fixes bugs including an XSS flaw. The Alice and Eleuteria coordinators left the federation. [github.com](https://github.com/RoboSats/robosats/releases/tag/v0.8.8-alpha)
