## Regulation & Policy

#### Brazil orders reporting of self-custody crypto transfers above $10K

The central bank says wallets under full user control "can reduce the availability of information for monitoring and risk assessment purposes," and wants the trades folded into its AML/CTF oversight. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/brazil-orders-reporting-10k-self-custody-wallet-trades/)

#### SEC Commissioner Hester 'Crypto Mom' Peirce advocates privacy-preserving technology

The outgoing regulator criticized how supervisors collect data on crypto users, arguing oversight need not come at the cost of user privacy. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/hester-peirce-advocates-privacy-tech)

#### Bank of Russia calls crypto a source of cash of "unestablished origin"

Bogdan Shablya, head of the central bank's financial monitoring service, named markets and crypto as the main sources of untraceable cash and signaled tighter bank checks on where clients' money comes from. [bits.media](https://bits.media/v-banke-rossii-kriptovalyutu-nazvali-istochnikom-nalichnykh-neustanovlennogo-proiskhozhdeniya/)

## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Sequans exits its Bitcoin treasury strategy, selling the last 314 BTC

The French semiconductor maker has fully unwound a treasury that once held more than 3,200 BTC, as more companies scale back their holdings. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sequans-exits-bitcoin-treasury-strategy-after-selling-remaining-314-btc)

#### Institutions held their bitcoin through the crash — and some bought more, report finds

New research finds bitcoin is the one crypto asset large investors agree on, with allocations surviving the drawdown rather than being cut. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/institutions-held-bitcoin-through-crash)

## Lightning & L2

#### Lightning Labs discloses critical bug that marked canceled invoices as paid

Merchants could have been misled into releasing goods for free. The flaw affected older software; fixes for tapd and lnd shipped in 2025. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/lightning-labs-discloses-critical-bug-marking-canceled-invoices-paid-risking-free-product-delivery/)

#### Denial-of-service vulnerabilities fixed in Eclair v0.14.0

Two bugs let a peer exhaust a node's memory: bit-by-bit feature parsing burned ~300 MB of heap per init message, and retired zlib channel queries inflated 64 KB into 64 MB. Node operators should upgrade. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/disclosure-dos-vulnerabilities-fixed-in-eclair-v0-14-0/2914) [github.com](https://github.com/ACINQ/eclair/blob/972dfe995de0a24e8b496c6cf7144e7db377d51d/docs/release-notes/eclair-v0.14.0.md)

#### ZEUS v13.2.2 ships with embedded LND v0.21.3-beta and moves iOS wallet data off iCloud Keychain

Older versions stored seeds and credentials in the iCloud-synced keychain; the release relocates them to device-only storage and silently deletes the synced copies. Also adds new swap providers and Cashu and NWC fixes. [github.com](https://github.com/ZeusLN/zeus/releases/tag/v13.2.2)

## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin's 'last resort' quantum-safe solution just got 79% cheaper, says StarkWare

A weeklong optimization contest cut the estimated GPU cost of preparing an experimental quantum-resistant transaction to under $67. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/bitcoins-last-resort-quantum-safe-solution-just-got-79-cheaper-starkware)

#### \[\[alloc\] init\] releases Shielded Bitcoin proposal for private transactions

The proposal would give Bitcoin transactions strong privacy guarantees. It is a design put forward for discussion, not shipped software. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/technical/alloc-init-releases-shielded-bitcoin-proposal-for-private-bitcoin-transactions)

#### Silent Payments light client measurements: taproot-only filters 17.63x smaller than BIP-158

Across 255,434 mainnet blocks, a taproot-only filter costs 0.33 GB versus 5.78 GB for the basic BIP-158 filter. Cut-through plus a dust limit shrinks the restore payload from 15.08 GB to as little as 0.48 GB. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/silent-payments-light-client-protocol/891#post_17) [github.com](https://github.com/bitsagarob/silentpayments-measurements)

## Security & Privacy

#### BitBox newsletter email addresses exposed in breach at provider Brevo

Attackers abused an SSO flaw to reach over 100 Brevo accounts, downloaded BitBox's contact list and sent phishing mail asking for recovery words. Only email addresses were exposed; wallets, the app and shop orders were untouched. [blog.bitbox.swiss](https://blog.bitbox.swiss/en/email-address-exposure-at-newsletter-provider-brevo/) [bitbox.swiss](http://bitbox.swiss/newsletter)

#### Wasabi will shut down its indexer server on March 1, 2027

The server supplies compact block filters to older Wasabi versions, so users on those releases will need to upgrade before the date. [x.com](https://x.com/wasabiwallet/status/2102754384849543175)
