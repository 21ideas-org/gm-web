Your morning Bitcoin brief: reports of BTCPay Server hot wallets being swept by attackers, bitcoin's worst October since 2014, mining revenue at an 11-month high and other bitcoin news

## Security & Privacy

#### BTCPay Server says it is receiving reports of hot wallets being swept by attackers

The team advises operators to shut down instances until its investigation is complete. AQUA and Samson Mow suggest the SamRock plugin with AQUA Wallet as a mitigation, since on-chain and Liquid payments then settle directly to the user's own wallet. [x.com](https://x.com/BtcpayServer/status/2117020340815819262) [x.com](https://x.com/sesi_the_man/status/2117033039771697301)

#### umbrelOS 2.0.1 addresses Linux kernel network vulnerability CVE-2026-98432

On x86 devices, including Umbrel Home and Umbrel Pro, the update ships a fixed kernel; on Raspberry Pi, where none is available yet, umbrelOS blocks the traffic needed to trigger the flaw. It also fixes a HEIC decoder flaw and drops recently distrusted CAs. [github.com](https://github.com/getumbrel/umbrel/releases/tag/2.0.1)

#### Binance allows limited withdrawals two days after a hack of about $6.3 billion

The exchange says it covered about 95% of the stolen assets from its own resources. Binance said it has since processed $4.649 billion in withdrawals, with deposits leaving net outflows of about $3.64 billion. [bits.media](https://bits.media/binance-razreshila-klientam-ogranichennyy-vyvod-sredstv/) [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/binance-processes-4-65b-in-withdrawals-across-15-networks-after-hack/)

#### OneKey founder suggests BlackRock's IBIT could be North Korean hackers' next target

Yishi Wang, founder of hardware wallet maker OneKey, speculated after the Binance hack that iShares Bitcoin Trust, the world's largest exchange-traded bitcoin fund, may be targeted next. [bits.media](https://bits.media/osnovatel-onekey-nazval-sleduyushchuyu-krupnuyu-tsel-khakerov-posle-binance/)

## Technology & Development

#### waxwing says BABE sharply cuts on-chain dispute costs in the Lichen L2 design

Replacing BitVMX with BABE cuts a full dispute to about 4–5 transactions from 20–25 and on-chain cost to around 30 kvB from 200. The author is deploying a hub on mutinynet and estimates a single hub could support tens of thousands of users. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/eed59d04-8620-4b04-b97e-48d2432e74fbn@googlegroups.com/) [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/eed59d04-8620-4b04-b97e-48d2432e74fbn@googlegroups.com/1.1-a.txt)

#### 0xB10C: rune-mint transaction chains likely behind compact block reconstruction slump

His current understanding: someone pushed chains of ~100 vB rune-mint transactions to nodes unsolicited while another party burned them, which he suspects caused mempool differences. The activity stopped on October 18, and reconstruction rates improved. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/stats-on-compact-block-reconstructions/1052?page=4#post_63) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/u/m4ycon)

#### Specter DIY hit by board shortage as team works on ESP32-P4-based Specter 2

The STM32F469I-DISCO boards Specter DIY uses are sold out at major distributors, with lead times extending into 2027. Specter 2 is in development; a newsletter will announce when it becomes available. [x.com](https://x.com/SpecterDIY/status/2116912589821313375) [specter.solutions](https://specter.solutions/)
