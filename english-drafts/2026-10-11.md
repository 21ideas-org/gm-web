## Security & Privacy

#### Ledger confirms hidden implant in one affected user's hardware wallet

The find lends weight to suspicions of a supply chain attack via Southeast Asian reseller CryptoBilis. Lookonchain analysts reported an investor lost 80 BTC ($6.6 million) after moving coins to a Ledger wallet bought from that reseller. ## Regulation & Policy

#### French committee approves proposed tax on crypto-to-stablecoin conversions from 2027

The proposal would tax conversions from any cryptocurrency to stablecoins, closing what legislators call a "loophole". The National Assembly plenary must still discuss and approve it before it becomes law. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/french-committee-approves-tax-on-stablecoin-conversions-from-2027/)

## Mining

#### Russian Energy Ministry says miners can be cut off from the grid at any time

According to the ministry, miners will be able to connect to the grid only as "fourth reliability category" consumers, who can be disconnected at any time without prior approval. [bits.media](https://bits.media/rossiyskikh-maynerov-smogut-v-lyuboy-moment-otklyuchat-ot-seti-resheniem-vlastey/)

#### Luxor's reported 6–13% annualized bitcoin yield hinges on mining delivery

According to CryptoSlate, Luxor's paired forwards can lock in gross BTC receipts, but mining delivery, counterparty performance and financing terms still determine repayment. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoins-reported-6-13-mining-yield-still-carries-miner-credit-risk/)

#### Draft BIP proposes a standard format for co-signer spending policies

Co-signers that sign only when a condition holds can emulate covenants today.
