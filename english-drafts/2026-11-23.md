## Regulation & Policy

#### UK to bar crypto exchanges from making markets on their own platforms as Bitcoin rules change

Firms making markets in spot bitcoin on qualifying platforms are exempt from principal-dealing permission for that activity; separate venue rules apply. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/uk-to-ban-crypto-exchanges-from-making-markets-on-their-own-platforms-as-bitcoin-rules-change/)

#### Wallets linked to hedge fund Abraxas Capital receive about $1 billion in BTC as price dips

Wallets associated with Abraxas Capital Management received approximately 17,597 BTC through exchange withdrawals as bitcoin fell below $60,000, coinciding with a sharp recovery in the firm's tracked bitcoin balances. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/bitcoins-price-dip-attracts-a-whale-as-hedge-fund-scoops-up-1b/)

## Mining

#### Bitcoin hashrate reclaims 1 ZH/s even as mining profits sour

After shedding roughly 100 EH/s while bitcoin's price fell to levels last seen in early September 2024, hashrate rebounded to the 1,000 EH/s threshold. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/bitcoin-mining-profits-turn-ugly-despite-hashrates-return-above-1-zh-s/)

#### Solo bitcoin miner wins roughly $190,000 block against 130 million-to-one odds

The miner, running just 8 TH/s, struck on Nov. 22, 2026, joining a string of unlikely solo winners as the industry faces punishing economics and depressed prices. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/solo-bitcoin-miner-wins-190k-jackpot-against-130-million-to-one-odds/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Bisq v2.1.16 patches Java4Shell vulnerability; update required to keep trading

The release updates Bisq's bundled Java 21 runtime to fix CVE-2026-114938, which could let an attacker run code on affected systems, and includes the latest Tor security release. Bisq urges updating as soon as possible, even if not trading. [github.com](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq2/releases/tag/v2.1.16)

## Technology & Development

#### Haller-1 open-source AI models read handwritten BIP39 seed phrases, running experimentally on SeedSigner

Released as v1 by @pizza\_bitcoiner, the four small networks (714k parameters, 1.5 MB) run fully offline on a Raspberry Pi Zero, usually reading a photo in under 10 seconds. Weights, datasets and the SeedSigner proof-of-concept are open source. [x.com](https://x.com/pizza_bitcoiner/status/2124553407159472599) [x.com](https://x.com/sesi_the_man/status/2124576930569822570)

#### Zawy floats a difficulty-adjustment tweak to tighten chain-length bounds under BIP-54 timewarp fixes

Zawy argues that difficulty adjustment can't keep up with persistent hashrate increases, letting an attacker cheat on chain time and work; shorter averaging windows help at a cost in stability. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bounds-on-chain-length-with-bip-54-timewarp-fixes/2899?page=2#post_31)
