## Security & Privacy

#### Keystone to destroy 18,835 hardware wallets after hackers steal a device authentication key

The website breach has widened: Keystone has suspended shipments, and personal information of nearly 26,000 customers was exposed. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/keystone-breach-deepens-18835-crypto-hardware-wallets-face-destruction/)

#### Passport hardware wallet owners receive phishing emails sent from Foundation's official domains

Foundation, the maker of Passport, told customers that attackers sent the emails from its domains after Cloudflare API keys were compromised. [bits.media](https://bits.media/vladeltsy-apparatnykh-koshelkov-passport-poluchili-fishingovuyu-rassylku/)

#### BTCPay Server 2.4.6 released with broad security hardening

Hardens API permissions, untrusted-input handling, generated links and browser actions, and updates .NET to fix a Brotli decoder flaw (CVE-2026-111497). Breaking: the legacy per-invoice notificationEmail option is removed in favour of email rules. [github.com](https://github.com/btcpayserver/btcpayserver/releases/tag/v2.4.6) [x.com](https://x.com/BtcpayServer/status/2114742606660063272)

#### Wasabi Wallet v2.8.5 brings security updates and drops payjoin sending

Updates dependencies to address disclosed vulnerabilities, adds stricter transaction verification against malicious servers and fixes coinjoin edge cases. Sending BIP78 payjoin transactions was removed to reduce attack surface. [github.com](https://github.com/WalletWasabi/WalletWasabi/releases/tag/v2.8.5)

## Regulation & Policy

#### South Korea weighs fourth delay of crypto tax, with 2029 start tied to US data sharing

The 22% tax would move from January 2027 to 2029 so authorities can access US trading data once Washington joins the OECD's crypto-asset reporting framework. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/south-korea-weighs-fourth-delay-crypto-tax)

#### Bank of Russia: Russians can move their own crypto to digital depositories without investment-risk tests

The central bank said a transfer from a personal wallet to a digital depository is not treated as a purchase if the owner doesn't change, citing the law on state regulation of crypto operations. [bits.media](https://bits.media/rossiyanam-razreshat-perevodit-svoyu-kriptovalyutu-bez-testov-na-ponimanie-riska-investitsiy/)

## Lightning & L2

#### Dave Lund proposes removing Lightning's shachain requirement to enable K-of-N nodes, with two implementations

His option\_independent\_secrets channel\_type add-on lets per-commitment secrets be generated jointly across signers. Forks of lnd (merged) and Core Lightning (in review) interoperate. Built on BLAKE2b Bitcoin, not SHA256d; he says it carries over. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/towards-a-k-of-n-lightning-network-node/2395#post_7) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-8825-how-it-differs-from-the-original-proposal-1)

## Technology & Development

#### Liana v16.0 release candidate adds translations and Taproot by default

The installer enables Taproot by default and the GUI gains French, Italian and Portuguese translations. Breaking: v16 migrates the database, so wallets opened with it can't be opened in v15 or earlier; descriptor backups move to BIP138 files. [github.com](https://github.com/wizardsardine/liana/releases/tag/v16.0rc1)
