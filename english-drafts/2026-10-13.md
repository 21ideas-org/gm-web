#### Strategy buys $20.8 million in bitcoin

Last week the largest public corporate bitcoin holder bought 250 BTC for about $20.8 million, and also bought back $192 million of its STRC preferred shares from investors. [bits.media](https://bits.media/strategy-kupila-bitkoiny-na-20-8-mln/)

#### South Korea's Jeonbuk bank starts offering bitcoin-backed loans

Jeonbuk, a subsidiary bank of JB Financial Group, has begun offering bitcoin-backed loans to corporate clients; the bitcoin pledged as collateral is held by a third-party custodian. [bits.media](https://bits.media/yuzhnokoreyskiy-bank-nachinaet-predlagat-obespechennye-bitkoinom-kredity/)
