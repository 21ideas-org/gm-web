## Price & Market

#### Bitcoin falls toward $57K on US tariff fears and hot PCE inflation data

Bitcoin hit an intraday low of $57,209 as US tariff fears and hotter-than-expected PCE inflation weighed on risk assets, sending crypto liquidations to $579 million. It is down nearly 25% since the start of November. ## Regulation & Policy

#### Strategic Bitcoin Reserve bill clears House committee, heads to the floor

Rep. Nick Begich's bill would write the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve into law with a 20-year ban on selling its bitcoin; it is now eligible for a House floor vote. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/strategic-bitcoin-reserve-bill-house-floor)

#### Ukraine's parliament passes crypto regulation and tax bill

The Verkhovna Rada approved a bill regulating virtual assets along with Tax Code amendments, by 271 votes to eight. The law is set to take effect on July 1, 2027. [bits.media](https://bits.media/ukrainskaya-rada-odobrila-zakonoproekt-o-regulirovanii-i-nalogakh-dlya-kriptovalyut/)

#### Switzerland approves 57 partners for crypto tax data sharing from 2028

The partner jurisdictions, including all EU states and the UK, will take part in automatic crypto tax data exchanges under the OECD's Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF). [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/switzerland-57-countries-crypto-tax)

#### Russia's Finance Ministry drafts bill to raise fines for illegal crypto exchange

The proposed higher fines target illegal crypto trading, primarily by operators without a license or not listed in the relevant state register. [bits.media](https://bits.media/v-rossii-sobirayutsya-uvelichit-shtrafy-za-nelegalnyy-kriptoobmen/)

## Mining

#### CleanSpark weighs selling more bitcoin than it mines after $987M loss

CleanSpark's CFO said the miner may sell more than it mines to reduce risk after a $987.1 million fiscal 2026 loss. It sold 2,102 BTC against 1,708 BTC mined in the September quarter; its shares hit a 52-week low. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/cleanspark-987-million-loss-fiscal-2026) [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/cleanspark-is-selling-bitcoin-to-fund-its-ai-expansion-as-annual-losses-approach-1-billion/)

#### Canaan's Nordic mining heat network could warm nearly 8,000 homes

Canaan secured two more orders for 430 hydro-cooled Avalon units in the Nordic region, bringing its district heating portfolio to nearly 12 MW. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/canaan-expands-nordic-bitcoin-mining-heat-network-to-nearly-8000-homes/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Braiins warns of Stratum V1 attacks hijacking up to 1.9 EH/s of hashrate

The mining pool operator said a series of network hijacking attacks redirected up to 1.9 EH/s of hashrate by exploiting longstanding security weaknesses in the Stratum V1 mining protocol. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/braiins-warns-of-widespread-bitcoin-hashrate-hijacking-attacks/)

#### Hackers appear to have breached FinCEN's secure data transfer system

According to The Rage, the system is used to send sensitive financial reports to the US financial intelligence unit. [therage.co](https://www.therage.co/u-s-financial-intelligence-unit-hacked/)

#### ismaelsadeeq: Bitcoin Core wallet rebroadcast can link addresses to node IPs

Wallets re-announce unconfirmed transactions after 12–36 hours, a long-known leak; in a simulation, an active attack linked an address to its node 100% of the time. mzumsande notes #36482 only slightly raises the attack's cost. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/exploiting-wallet-rebroadcast-to-link-addresses-to-node-ips-and-more/3123) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-9136-introduction-1)

## Lightning & L2

#### Lightning release candidate could help recover funds after channel data loss

The 26.12 release candidate can help recover eligible funds if the operator still has key records and can get help from the peer. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-lightning-operators-could-recover-stuck-payments-even-after-losing-channel-data/)

#### Cooperative Lightning channel closes rise to 90.2% after forced-close spike

From Nov. 16 to 22, 90.2% of public channel closes were cooperative, up from 78.9% a week earlier and 47.6% in the week of the Nov. 8 forced closes. Forced closes fell 59%, from 357 to 145. [x.com](https://x.com/ambosstech/status/2125665868319543710) [amboss.space](https://amboss.space/stats)
