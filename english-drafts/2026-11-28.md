Your morning Bitcoin brief: VTB opens bitcoin trading to qualified investors in Russia, a 98-minute wait for a new block, Binance lifts withdrawal suspension after $6.3 billion hack and other bitcoin news

## Regulation & Policy

#### Russia's VTB opens bitcoin trading as central bank finalizes crypto test

VTB began letting qualified investors trade bitcoin in its app on Friday, a day after the Bank of Russia approved the knowledge test every crypto buyer must pass. SPB Exchange plans to open spot trading on Dec. 1. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/russia-vtb-bitcoin-trading-crypto-test)

#### Spain lets anti-money laundering unit freeze crypto accounts without a judge

A royal decree lets Sepblac, Spain's financial intelligence unit, freeze crypto, bank and payment accounts for up to five business days without a court order. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/spain-sepblac-freeze-crypto-accounts)

#### Japan weighs safeguards for bitcoin holders who keep their own keys

Japan's financial regulator is examining what exchanges can do to prevent scams and what could protect users after they withdraw funds. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/japan-weighs-safeguards-for-bitcoin-holders-who-keep-their-own-keys/)

#### European Commission proposes blocking deals with Kyrgyzstan-registered crypto platforms

The Commission proposed that all 27 member states bar brokers from transacting with any crypto provider registered in Kyrgyzstan. [bits.media](https://bits.media/evrokomissiya-khochet-zablokirovat-sdelki-s-kriptoplatformami-kyrgyzstana/)

## Mining

#### Bitcoin goes 98 minutes without a new block as mining pressure mounts

It was the longest block interval since July, amid declining hashrate and falling mining revenue. Mining still got harder: difficulty adjusted +7.61% at block 977,760, to 145.09T. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/bitcoin-98-minutes-without-new-block-mining-pressure-mounts/) [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-miners-are-shutting-down-machines-but-mining-just-got-even-harder/)

#### Vitalik Buterin proposes a way to verify hardware wallet security

The Ethereum co-founder argues that fully checking wallet chips requires access to GDS design files describing the chip's physical geometry. Ledger's CTO previously called the idea wishful thinking. ## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin Optech #433: flaw in older Bitcoin Core versions and a Core Lightning DoS disclosure

The newsletter describes a vulnerability affecting older versions of Bitcoin Core, summarizes a denial-of-service disclosure in Core Lightning and relays discussion about refreshing the BIP editor team. [bitcoinops.org](https://bitcoinops.org/en/newsletters/2026/11/27/)

#### conduition suggests a transaction extensions field for post-quantum witness data

The design sketch adds a new transaction field for protocol extensions, likely starting with post-quantum signature algorithms. Each extension commits recursively to its successor, building on @sipa's idea of chaining witness-style commitments. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/segwit-commitment-to-post-quantum-witness-data/2702#post_17) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/u/sipa)

#### Nunchuk 2.9.6 requires two inheritance keys to claim timelock plans

On-chain timelock inheritance plans now need two inheritance keys, so no single compromised device can take the inheritance. Optional fallbacks add a second heir or the user's own recovery. Windows builds are unsigned. [github.com](https://github.com/nunchuk-io/nunchuk-desktop/releases/tag/2.9.6)

## Lightning & L2

#### Core Lightning v26.12rc2 release candidate adds static Linux builds and xpay changes

The test build offers one static binary per architecture that runs on any Linux distribution, a new xpay maxparts cap for multi-part payments and two crash fixes. Developers ask users to try it on a test node. [github.com](https://github.com/ElementsProject/lightning/releases/tag/v26.12rc2) [x.com](https://x.com/Core_LN/status/2126460800873505146)
