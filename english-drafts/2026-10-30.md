## Security & Privacy

#### Billions flow out of Binance wallets in a suspected hack; bitcoin slips below $79,000

Analysts track up to $5.8 billion in potentially compromised assets. Binance says it is investigating a potential security issue and has reportedly suspended withdrawals; Tether and Circle froze about $1.8–2.4 billion in stablecoins. [bits.media](https://bits.media/s-binance-vyveli-aktivy-na-milliardy-dollarov-rynok-otreagiroval-padeniem-krupnykh-kriptovalyut/) [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/inside-the-5-8b-binance-hack-whats-confirmed-and-what-isnt/)

#### BTCPay Server 2.4.7 fixes 85 security findings

Reported via Anthropic's OSS Scanner program, the findings were rated 1 critical, 13 high, 52 medium and 19 low. Updating is highly recommended, especially for servers shared by multiple merchants. [github.com](https://github.com/btcpayserver/btcpayserver/releases/tag/v2.4.7)

#### Singapore police say tampered Ledger devices surfaced a month before the news broke

According to Singapore police, tampered Ledgers began appearing there on September 10. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/magazine/tampered-ledger-reported-in-singapore-a-month-before-news-broke-asia-express)

## Technology & Development

#### Blockstream says its researchers cut post-quantum signing on hardware wallets to about 3 seconds

According to Blockstream, the @blksresearch team brought signing time for post-quantum transactions down from over 20 seconds using hardware users already have. [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2115909357238206836) [blog.blockstream.com](https://blog.blockstream.com/hardware-wallets-post-quantum-signatures/)

#### Sparrow Wallet 2.5.6 updates bundled Tor and improves accessibility

Bundled Tor moves to v0.4.9.5 to meet client protocols the Tor network now requires, and Java v25.0.5 drops eMudhra root certificates. The release also expands screen reader and keyboard access. [github.com](https://github.com/sparrowwallet/sparrow/releases/tag/2.5.6)

#### Laz1m0v releases a 371-byte Tapscript multiplication routine that needs no soft fork

With OP\_MUL disabled since 2010, the witness-assisted construction multiplies values from 0 to 46,340 within Tapscript's 4-byte number limits. It ships with a Rocq proof, a Rust auditor and a regtest harness. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/multiplication-in-tapscript-today-371-bytes-no-soft-fork/3010) [github.com](https://github.com/Laz1mov/tapscript-mul16-unrolled)
