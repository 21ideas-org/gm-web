## Price & Market

## Mining

#### Bitcoin mining difficulty drops 9.13% as hashprice sinks to $28–$30

With bitcoin touching an intraday low of $61,947, hashprice hovered between $28 and $30 per PH/s of daily output, and difficulty fell 9.13% at block height 975744. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/bitcoin-mining-hits-breaking-point-as-difficulty-plunges-9-13/)

#### Luxor confirms hashrate hijack through its Commander platform, says the attack was AI-enabled

Luxor confirmed unauthorized redirection of mining hashrate via Commander, ending speculation about an alleged attack involving thousands of machines. The company says the incident was contained within two hours. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/luxor-confirms-bitcoin-mining-hack-and-reveals-ai-enabled-attack/)

#### Miner Weekly: some miners cut hashrate by 50% as AI revenue doubles

The Blocksbridge Consulting newsletter says the shift to AI compute is becoming visible in miners' income statements. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/miner-weekly-these-miners-cut-hashrate-50-as-ai-revenue-doubles/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Reported RoboSats Android flaw lets any co-installed app steal the robot identity

The reporter says an unvalidated Intent extra is injected into the WebView, exposing the robot token, Nostr and PGP keys, and demonstrated key theft on the v0.8.7 alpha APK. A second report flags a low-medium dispute race with no demonstrated fund loss.
