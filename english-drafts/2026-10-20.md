## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Strategy buys 267 BTC for $22.1 million

Bought October 12–18 at an average $82,900 per coin. Strategy also spent $193.1 million repurchasing STRC shares, raised its preferred-stock buyback authorization to $3 billion, and saw discretionary USD cash fall to $447.3 million. [bits.media](https://bits.media/strategy-dokupila-bitkoiny-na-22-1-mln/) [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/strategy-btc-22-million-buy-strc-buyback-193-million)

#### Super League stockholders approve Metaplanet's 2,100 BTC investment, paving the way for 'Superplanet'

The deal would give Japan's Metaplanet a majority stake in Nasdaq-listed Super League (about 96% per Bitcoin Magazine, 93.7% per Cointelegraph) and access to US capital markets, where it plans preferred-stock offerings to fund more bitcoin purchases. ## Technology & Development

#### btc-verified October update: connecting a formal consensus model to the real Bitcoin network

ProofOfKeags says the project, which builds a minimal formal model of Bitcoin consensus layer by layer, now aims to anchor it to the real network, as directly verifying Bitcoin Core's modern C++ is beyond the author's current resources. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/btc-verified-october-update-connecting-map-to-territory/2964) [proofofkeags.com](https://proofofkeags.com/research/2026-10-19-connecting-map-to-territory.html)

#### Zeeshan Ali Khan proposes opt-in exposure-aware transaction construction for wallets

A companion to bips PR #2294, the bitcoindev proposal would have opted-in wallets also cover outputs whose public keys a spend reveals and send change to a fresh, non-exposed condition, preserving resistance to BIP 360 long-exposure attacks. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/CAAa3hFNACEoE4KXi06SaqGM0qsOTrgT3HB6X2oiocp--r6-rmg@mail.gmail.com/) [github.com](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0360.mediawiki)
