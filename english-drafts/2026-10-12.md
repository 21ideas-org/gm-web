## Security & Privacy

#### User reportedly finds hidden hardware implant in a Ledger wallet in Europe

Social media reports suggest physically tampered Ledger devices may not be limited to Asia. Ledger has confirmed a hidden hardware implant in a wallet affected by the recent $93.4M exploit. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/user-exposes-hidden-hardware-implant-in-european-ledger-wallet/) [x.com](https://x.com/sesi_the_man/status/2109297558145441972)

#### Coldcard's official X account posts a phishing link after $100M fiasco

The Oct. 11 post falsely warned customers of a critical firmware vulnerability. Coldcard deleted it and says its credentials and two-factor authentication remain secure. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/coldcard-x-account-hit-by-phishing-scam-after-100m-fiasco/) [x.com](https://x.com/COLDCARDwallet/status/2109158742197440912)

#### Hardware wallet maker Tangem reports possible leak of customer data

Tangem cites unauthorized access to a third-party request-handling system; names and email addresses of at least some customers may have been compromised, the company said. [bits.media](https://bits.media/proizvoditel-kriptokoshelkov-tangem-obyavil-o-krazhe-personalnykh-dannykh-klientov/)
