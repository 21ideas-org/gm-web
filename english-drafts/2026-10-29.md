## Security & Privacy

#### Bitcoin Core discloses three easily discoverable vulnerabilities, ships fixes for four versions

Releases 32.1, 31.2, 30.4 and 29.5 patch two medium- and one low-severity flaw under an accelerated disclosure process. Three unrelated vulnerabilities pre-announced with 32.1 will be detailed in two weeks. #### Liquid Federation says it will move remaining 600 BTC to restore full LBTC backing

The Federation says a 2.5 BTC test transaction succeeded. BTC-to-LBTC swaps have resumed via Aqua, Bull Bitcoin and SideSwap, while peg-outs stay under enhanced security controls as an independent security audit nears completion. #### Two library bugs can make Bitcoin apps miscalculate PSBT fees

Apps that import PSBTs and rely on the vulnerable library's fee accounting can miscalculate miner fees and miss an excessive-fee warning, CryptoSlate reports. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-apps-risk-massive-fee-drain-and-2-library-bugs-are-blamed/)
