## Security & Privacy

#### LDK 0.1.13, 0.2.7 and 0.3-rc3 fix several security vulnerabilities, including fund theft

Anyone running LDK in production with untrusted channel counterparties is urged to upgrade today. The upcoming 0.3, still a release candidate, adds splicing improvements, async signing, LSPS1 service support and BOLT 12 payer proofs. [x.com](https://x.com/lightningdevkit/status/2106055694210519129) [git.rust-bitcoin.org](https://git.rust-bitcoin.org/lightningdevkit/rust-lightning/releases/tag/v0.1.13)

#### Nearly $2.7 billion lost in digital asset incidents in 2026

CertiK counts 658 incidents through September. [atlas21.com](https://atlas21.com/certik-reports-nearly-2-7bn-lost-in-2026-security-incidents/)

#### Bisq 1.10.9 fixes vulnerabilities found in a security audit

Updating is required to keep trading. The release also validates BSQ swap inputs and signatures on both sides of a trade and updates Tor to 0.4.9.13. [github.com](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq/releases/tag/v1.10.9)

#### Masked robbers threaten pregnant wife in UK crypto home attack

The couple say thieves forced their way into their home in December, threatened to kill the pregnant woman and her unborn child with a knife, and stole hundreds of thousands of pounds in cryptocurrency. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/masked-robbers-threaten-pregnant-wife-uk-crypto-home-attack/)

## Regulation & Policy

#### IMF praises El Salvador but still pushes it to scale back its Bitcoin project

The International Monetary Fund approved a $139 million disbursement to El Salvador while still wanting the country to scale back its Bitcoin project. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/imf-praises-el-salvador-but-blasts-bitcoin)

#### Illinois moves to delay its crypto transaction tax

The US state agreed to push back the start of tax collection on cryptocurrency transactions from January 1 to July 1, 2027. [bits.media](https://bits.media/vlasti-shtata-illinoys-khotyat-otlozhit-sbor-naloga-na-kriptotranzaktsii/)

#### US Congress weighs rules for banks dealing in crypto

After the CLARITY crypto market bill failed, Congress is considering alternative ways to regulate how banks work with digital assets, the Congressional Research Service reported. [bits.media](https://bits.media/v-kongresse-ssha-predlagayut-ustanovit-bankam-pravila-raboty-s-kriptovalyutoy/)

#### South Africa's Absa reportedly becomes the continent's first bank to custody bitcoin

According to reports first published by Bloomberg, the Johannesburg-based lender is the first African bank to custody bitcoin and will serve institutional clients. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/absa-first-african-bank-to-custody-bitcoin)

#### Bitcoin pays no coupon: Saylor details where Strategy gets the dollars

Michael Saylor outlined how Strategy manages cash, financing costs and different shareholder rights to pay investors dollar dividends while continuing to accumulate bitcoin. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/bitcoin-pays-no-coupon-saylor-details-where-strategy-gets-the-dollars/)

#### New bitcoin treasury firm already plans to sell BTC

#### COLDCARD firmware 5.6.3 and 1.5.3Q add Codex32 and Shamir backups

Mk4, Mk5 and Q users can split the wallet already on the device into two to nine Codex32 (BIP-93) Shamir shares without moving funds. The release also adds PSBTv2 locktime support plus signing and parsing fixes.
