## Regulation & Policy

#### CFTC opens its crypto rulebook for comment as Chairman Selig tells industry to 'build here'

A 60-day public comment period now covers proposed rules for retail crypto commodity transactions, part of a potentially sweeping US market framework. Selig paired it with a direct pitch to builders to stay in the US. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/cftc-opens-crypto-rulebook-selig-tells-industry-build-here/)

#### Circle and Tether criticise MiCA's bank reserve rules

The stablecoin issuers object to MiCA's mandatory bank-deposit requirement for reserves; Circle proposes replacing it with a liquidity standard. [atlas21.com](https://atlas21.com/circle-and-tether-criticise-mica-bank-reserve-rules/)

#### Japan sanctions crypto exchange Garantex

Japanese authorities added the Russian exchange to their expanded sanctions list, alongside other restrictions on Russian entities. [bits.media](https://bits.media/yaponiya-vvela-sanktsii-protiv-kriptobirzhi-garantex/)

## Technology & Development

#### BlueWallet v8.0.2 released for iOS and Android

Fixes a BIP84 import bug that could send funds to a legacy address instead of native SegWit and repairs RBF for HD Bech32 wallets; adds BIP39 word suggestions, BIP329 label import/export and PSBT previews on iOS. [x.com](https://x.com/bluewalletio/status/2107158327075512757) [donate.bluewallet.io](https://donate.bluewallet.io/)

#### StartOS v0.4.0.2 adds a trusted-network switch and hardware virtualization for services

Users on 0.3.5.1 must follow the 0.4.0 update guide precisely to avoid data loss. The release also lets devices on routed private networks reach services' ports, so a miner on an IoT network can reach a pool's stratum port. [github.com](https://github.com/Start9Labs/start-technologies/releases/tag/start-os/v0.4.0.2)
