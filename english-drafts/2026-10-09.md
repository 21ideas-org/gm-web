## Technology & Development

#### Robin Linus's Bitcoin Poker protocol revives Satoshi's abandoned poker idea

Satoshi Nakamoto left an unfinished poker interface in Bitcoin's original software nearly 18 years ago; researcher Robin Linus's cryptographic design lets two people play Texas Hold'em without a dealer. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/satoshis-abandoned-poker-experiment-returns-with-a-bitcoin-twist/)

#### Cassis, a simple scheme for multi-hop HTLC routing across Bitcoin layers

Cassis aims to implement a previously voiced idea: any layer that can express an HTLC can be a hop. Payments chain HTLCs across layers such as Fedimint or Cashu mints, locked to one SHA-256 hash, with timeouts decreasing toward the receiver. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/cassis-a-new-simple-scheme-for-generic-multi-layer-multi-hop-htlc-routing/2952) [docs.arkadeos.com](https://docs.arkadeos.com/intents/overview)
