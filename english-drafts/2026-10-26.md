## Security & Privacy

#### Electrum patches a wallet vulnerability exploited to steal bitcoin

Electrum released urgent update 4.8.3. Attackers swapped legitimate payments for fraudulent transactions that wallet owners could mistakenly authorize. [bits.media](https://bits.media/khakery-ispolzovali-uyazvimost-v-koshelke-electrum-dlya-krazhi-bitkoinov/)

#### Disclosed Core Lightning bug lets a self-referential channel announcement crash nodes

Versions v25.02 through v26.04 abort on a channel\_announcement naming the same node twice and crash again on every restart until the record is removed by hand. The attacker needs only an unspent 2-of-2 output. Upgrade to v26.04.1 or later. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/cln-self-referential-channel-announcement-dos-disclosure/2974) [nishantbansal2003.github.io](https://nishantbansal2003.github.io/posts/CLN-Self-Referential-Channel-Announcement-DoS/)

## Mining

#### Bitcoin's 7-day hashrate returns to 1 ZH/s after nearly 8 months

Miners are again performing more than 1 sextillion calculations per second to secure the network and compete for newly minted bitcoin. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/mining/bitcoins-7-day-hashrate-returns-to-1-zh-s-after-nearly-8-months/)

#### Paolo Ardoino wants AI to run Bitcoin mines, raising the question of who may act

#### Heatbit's new Bitwater device turns a water heater into a bitcoin miner
