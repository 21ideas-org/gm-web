## Security & Privacy

#### Liquid Network announces final stages of recovery after massive hack

Bitcoin Magazine reports the network is running again a month after hackers took 4,000 bitcoins. @AquaBitcoin says peg-ins and peg-outs via @side\_swap are processing at 1:1, while Lightning-to-Liquid swaps are still being restored. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/liquid-network-announces-recovery) [x.com](https://x.com/Liquid_BTC/status/2112526531096453606)

#### ACINQ releases Eclair v0.14.5 with security hardening fixes

ACINQ highly recommends upgrading, as some of the issues can be exploited by malicious nodes. The release is compatible with previous versions: stop Eclair, upgrade and restart, with no need to close channels. [github.com](https://github.com/ACINQ/eclair/releases/tag/v0.14.5) [x.com](https://x.com/acinq_co/status/2112609943048138760)

#### Thieves hijack Ngrave alerts and walk away with $2.56M

A fake security alert sent users straight to thieves, who drained roughly $2.56 million by exploiting a trusted notification system without cracking the hardware itself, news.bitcoin.com reports. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/crypto-thieves-hijack-ngrave-alerts-and-walk-away-with-2-56m/)

## Technology & Development

#### Cost-bounded transaction filtering language implemented in the satd Bitcoin node

The non-Turing-complete policy language in the Rust node lets runners choose which transactions to relay or include in block templates without affecting consensus. Matches are quarantined, not rejected; filters that may hit Lightning channel closures are refused by default. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/cost-bounded-transaction-filtering-language/2966#post_1) [github.com](https://github.com/epochbtc/satd)
