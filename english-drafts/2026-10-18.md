## Regulation & Policy

#### France's National Assembly narrowly adopts proposed tax on crypto-to-stablecoin swaps

If passed, the measure would levy a 31.4% capital gains tax each time French holders exchange a cryptocurrency for a stablecoin. Analysts agree this would prompt holders to consider alternatives for parking their assets. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/french-assembly-narrowly-adopts-stablecoin-swap-tax-proposal/)
