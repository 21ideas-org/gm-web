## Lightning & L2

#### Core Lightning v26.12 release candidate 1 is out

There was no v26.09: that slot went to vulnerability fixes in v26.06.x, so the candidate carries 832 commits. The pay command will give way to xpay in v27.03. Anyone upgrading from v26.06.x should read the changelog carefully. [github.com](https://github.com/ElementsProject/lightning/releases/tag/v26.12rc1)

#### Lightning node operators commit 603 BTC to new public channels, up 14% week over week

Per @ambosstech data for Nov. 9–15, fewer channels opened (1,396 vs 1,473) but they were larger, averaging 0.43 BTC vs 0.36 BTC. Forced closes fell from 1,723 to 357 after the Nov. 8 spike; public capacity held at 3,238 BTC. [x.com](https://x.com/ambosstech/status/2123129139192639661) [amboss.space](https://amboss.space/stats)

#### German court offers to drop Movie2k case in exchange for 40,000 bitcoin

A Leipzig judge proposed dropping tax evasion and money laundering charges if the main defendant hands over 40,000 BTC (about $2.5 billion) and pays $41.67 million in victim compensation, leaving him about 16,000 BTC worth roughly $1.02 billion. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/german-court-offers-drop-movie2k-case-40000-bitcoin) [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/german-judge-offers-drop-movie2k-charges-2-5-billion-bitcoin/)
