## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Twenty One Capital buys another $10 million in bitcoin

The NYSE-listed US company acquired 172.25 BTC at an average price of about $58,054 per coin. [bits.media](https://bits.media/kompaniya-twenty-one-capital-dokupila-bitkoiny-na-10-mln/)

Officials say privately that their institutions hold gold rather than tokenized gold for self-custody, which the report calls the same reason bitcoin is valuable. ## Mining

#### Bitmain's challengers pull back from mining hardware sales as BTC slumps and AI grows

Companies that set out to challenge Bitmain's dominance have either stopped selling to external clients or sharply reduced sales, so efforts relevant to Bitcoin's resilience now appear to be on pause. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/opinion-and-analysis/bitcoin-miners-fewer-hardware-sellers-2026-btc-tanked-ai-grew/)

#### Bitfarms paid an $11.9M tariff bill four years after delivery

Bitfarms believed the machines it brought into Washington state in 2021 were made in Malaysia, as its supplier said, and gave US Customs documents supporting that claim. Four years later, it paid the government $11.9 million. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/one-bitcoin-miner-paid-an-11-9m-tariff-bill-four-years-after-delivery/)

## Security & Privacy

#### COLDCARD says independent Keylabs review found its seed-generation fix sound within scope

The July 2026 defect made COLDCARD firmware use a software generator instead of the STM32 hardware RNG. Keylabs reviewed Mk5 5.6.2/5.6.3 and Q 1.5.2Q/1.5.3Q; the review neither changes migration guidance nor repairs seeds already generated on vulnerable firmware. [blog.coinkite.com](https://blog.coinkite.com/keylabs-coldcard-seed-generation-review/) [keylabs.io](https://keylabs.io/)

#### IOSCO warns of quantum risk to blockchains as Bitcoin developers weigh fixes

In its latest quantum computing report, IOSCO says networks including Bitcoin could struggle to prepare before a post-quantum world because upgrades require coordinated action. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/iosco-warns-quantum-risk-blockchains-bitcoin-devs-weigh-fixes/)

## Technology & Development

#### Node Attestation Protocol proposed to survey support among Bitcoin's economic nodes

bkeroack's proposal lets participants publish key-value attestations backed by proof of UTXO control via a new P2P message; an aggregator weights them by coin value, aiming to survey support beyond miner signaling for contentious changes. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/node-attestation-protocol/3100) [bitcoin.com](http://bitcoin.com/)

#### Draft Multisig Session Protocol for remote multisig setup and signing seeks review

The draft by onedigitmoney, author of Keylay, lets remote cosigners authenticate over a relay treated as hostile and each assemble the descriptor themselves, so no coordinator can substitute keys. v0.1 supports two clients at a time. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/multisig-session-protocol-draft-spec-for-remote-multisig-setup-and-signing-needs-review/3103) [keylay.org](https://keylay.org/)

#### ariard asks whether BIP 54 could enable a minor, hypothetical 'slow-sliding MTP' attack

While reviewing BIP 54, ariard asks whether its timestamp rule for blocks at heights divisible by 2016 could let whoever sets block N-1's time affect the next block's validity, while judging it not significant enough to outweigh BIP 54's security benefit. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/open-question-on-a-hypothetical-minor-slow-sliding-mtp-attack-introduced-by-bip-54/3101) [groups.google.com](https://groups.google.com/g/bitcoindev/c/tKETFYnEtng)
