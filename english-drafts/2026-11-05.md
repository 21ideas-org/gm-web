## Regulation & Policy

#### House Democrats weigh their own crypto market structure bill with a bigger SEC role

After their sweep of Congress, House Democrats are discussing a bill built on Rep. Maxine Waters' rejected Clarity Act amendments, with a bigger SEC role, a smaller CFTC role and new ethics limits. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/house-democrats-crypto-market-structure)

#### Democrats reportedly plan a probe into the Trump family's crypto business

According to Bloomberg and the Financial Times, Democrats, now controlling both chambers of Congress, aim to open an investigation into the business's legality within the first 100 hours after the new Congress convenes on January 3. [bits.media](https://bits.media/kongressmeny-demokraty-gotovyat-rassledovanie-kriptobiznesa-trampa/)

#### South Korea's ruling party seeks to delay the crypto income tax

Authorities had planned to start taxing crypto investors' gains on January 1, 2027; the Democratic Party proposed first drafting legislation for the crypto industry. [bits.media](https://bits.media/v-yuzhnoy-koree-khotyat-otlozhit-nachalo-sbora-naloga-na-kriptovalyuty/)

#### Block sets aside $764 million for DOJ settlement as its bitcoin profit halves

Block agreed in principle to a $764 million deferred-prosecution deal over anti-money-laundering failures at Cash App, a sum that exceeds its $566 million third-quarter profit. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/block-xyz-sets-aside-764-million)

## Price & Market

#### Bitcoin holds near $80,000 as Democratic sweep sparks a short squeeze

Bitcoin rebounded from an election-night low of $75,600 amid a short squeeze, a stock rally and a 9% drop in oil after Democrats swept Congress. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/markets/bitcoin-price-holds-near-80000-short)

#### Glassnode: bitcoin idles at support after a roughly 10% pullback

Bitcoin fell back from its September high into the levels it broke above in August. Short-term holders went from nearly all in profit to mostly at a loss, while spot volume holds above its pre-pump level. [research.glassnode.com](https://research.glassnode.com/the-week-onchain-week-44-2026/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Matt Corallo flags a critical Linux kernel flaw; Lightning Labs ships an LND mitigation

Corallo highlighted CVE-2026-98432 on October 30: a use-after-free in the Linux kernel's IPv4 TCP handshake code, scored CVSS 9.8. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/matt-corallo-flags-critical-linux-kernel-flaw-lightning-labs-ships-lnd-mitigation)

#### Core Lightning 26.06.10 point release patches reported vulnerabilities

Security fixes cover dual funding and splicing, onchaind HTLC resolution, gossip limits and more; tests for the fixes are temporarily withheld. Developers strongly recommend upgrading as soon as practical. [github.com](https://github.com/ElementsProject/lightning/releases/tag/v26.06.10)

#### Keystone now erases customers' personal data 90 days after purchase

Keystone says it confirmed on Nov 3 that personal data in records older than 90 days was erased; a daily workflow now handles new orders via Shopify. It warns scammers may pose as Keystone: never share your recovery phrase. [x.com](https://x.com/KeystoneWallet/status/2117996631308288113) [keyst.one](http://keyst.one/)
