## Regulation & Policy

#### EU joins 8 nations in signing Warsaw Protocol on crypto crime

The treaty updates the Warsaw Convention to cover crypto assets, expands the powers of financial intelligence units over them and aims to cut response times for information requests between countries. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/eu-joins-8-nations-to-sign-warsaw-protocol-on-crypto-crime/)

## Technology & Development

#### SwiftSync proof of concept: speeding up initial block download with pre-generated hints

Floresta has added a SwiftSync implementation that uses the implicit-deletions technique for Utreexo. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/swiftsync-speeding-up-ibd-with-pre-generated-hints-poc/1562#post_19) [github.com](https://github.com/getfloresta/Floresta/pull/1115)

#### AdamISZ on variable payout amounts for BABE, as applied to Lichen

BABE adapts witness encryption to Groth16 proof verification, so that bitcoin can be locked to be unlockable only by someone holding a valid SNARK proof. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/variable-payout-amounts-for-babe-as-applied-to-lichen/2963) [eprint.iacr.org](https://eprint.iacr.org/2026/065)

#### Fulcrum 2.1.4 makes some history lookups up to 5x-10x faster

The maintenance release speeds up uncached history lookups for subscriptions and get\_history RPCs by looking up hashes in parallel; the speedup was discovered by @sethforprivacy, and all users are recommended to update. [github.com](https://github.com/cculianu/Fulcrum/releases/tag/v2.1.4)
