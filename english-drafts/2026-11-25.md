## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Strategy is now worth less than its bitcoin holdings

Michael Saylor's company has a market value near $35.7 billion, while the 851,305 BTC it held per its latest Form 8-K are worth roughly $51 billion. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/saylor-strategy-worth-less-than-bitcoin-holdings/)

#### Bitcoin lender Ledn moves US clients onshore with new custody accounts

The US custody account lets clients hold bitcoin, gold, stablecoins and dollars, with no trading fees and a 0.5% spread. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/ledn-launches-us-custody-accounts)

#### BlackRock drives Bitcoin ETFs to worst month on record as crypto fund withdrawals near $6 billion

#### Anchorage Digital sets up asset manager to offer bitcoin yield to clients

The subsidiary, 61st Parallel Asset Management, is expected to open by early December with a bitcoin-denominated fund that lets custody clients earn yield. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/anchorage-digital-bitcoin-yield-asset-manager)

#### UniCredit offers Italian savers bitcoin exposure, capping gains at 65%

## Regulation & Policy

#### CFTC staff let exchanges count bitcoin, ether and stablecoins as operating reserves

A Nov. 23 staff advisory allows exchanges and clearinghouses to count bitcoin, ether and payment stablecoins toward 12-month operating reserve requirements. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/cftc-opens-door-bitcoin-ether-stablecoins-operating-reserves/)
