## Regulation & Policy

#### US Senate seeks investigations into Tether and USDT use in Iran

A Senate subcommittee is calling for reviews of Tether's compliance with sanctions and anti-money-laundering rules. [atlas21.com](https://atlas21.com/us-senate-panel-seeks-inquiry-into-tether-and-usdt-in-iran/)

#### IRS scrutinizes a $7 billion crypto ETF tax strategy

The Treasury Department and IRS named digital assets as one area where fund managers may be stretching tax provisions, part of a wider push against fund structures designed to avoid taxable gains. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/a-7-billion-crypto-etf-plumbing-boom-just-ran-into-the-irs/)

#### Bank of Russia plans to add crypto to bank account statements

A draft directive published by the regulator would have commercial banks include cryptocurrency and digital financial asset holdings in the account and deposit certificates they issue to clients. [bits.media](https://bits.media/bank-rossii-obyazhet-deklarirovat-kriptovalyutu/)

## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin Core 32.0rc2 is now available

This is a release candidate for the next major version: if testing finds no critical problems, it will be tagged as v32.0. Draft release notes and a testing guide have been published. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bitcoin-core-32-0rc2-is-now-available/2921) [bitcoincore.org](https://bitcoincore.org/bin/bitcoin-core-32.0/test.rc2/)

#### BIP proposal for stateless PSBT coordination adds role-based access

Sean Carlin's Signing Room coordinator v3.4.0 introduces role-scoped capability tokens, so multisig observers such as auditors can verify a PSBT without being able to sign, while the relay stays account-free and stateless. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bip-proposal-stateless-psbt-coordination-blind-relay/2369#post_4) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-8664-updates-bip-specification-1)

#### Utreexo initial block download improvements show promising results

One developer cautions that making Utreexo depend on SwiftSync, another young technology, could delay its maturation, though it might also speed up SwiftSync's development. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/implicit-deletions-and-improvements-in-utreexo-ibd/2881#post_2) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/implicit-deletions-and-improvements-in-utreexo-ibd/2881#post_3)
