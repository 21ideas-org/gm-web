## Technology & Development

#### Developers bound the longest chain an attacker could build under BIP-54 timewarp fixes

Pieter Wuille and Zawy worked out how many blocks an adversary choosing timestamps freely could produce for a given amount of work — roughly 1,005,984 blocks in the worked example — which matters for the headers-presync DoS protection and the minchainwork value if BIP-54 is ever deployed and activated. The exchange also settles that the 2016/2014 difficulty bias applies only to honest miners using real timestamps, not to an attacker. #### Blink Wallet hit by attacker, ‘few dozen’ custodial accounts drained

The Lightning payments platform says an attacker reached a limited number of its custodial accounts and moved the funds, and that every affected account will be made whole. Only custodial balances were touched — self-custodial users hold their own keys.
