## Security & Privacy

#### lnd v0.21.6-beta and v0.20.7-beta mitigate Linux kernel flaw CVE-2026-98432

The releases disable MPTCP listeners by default, closing the demonstrated exploit path through lnd. It is a mitigation, not a fix: Linux operators should also disable MPTCP on the host and install a patched kernel. Windows and macOS are not affected. [github.com](https://github.com/lightningnetwork/lnd/releases/tag/v0.21.6-beta) [github.com](https://github.com/lightningnetwork/lnd/releases/tag/v0.20.7-beta)

#### Binance prepares phased withdrawal restart, unveils $6.3B hack recovery plan

Reopening depends on security checks, and the exchange committed company funds to replace stolen assets. ## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin Optech Newsletter #429: silent payments from multiparty wallets

The issue describes a protocol for creating silent payment transactions from multiparty wallets, the responsible disclosure of a denial-of-service vulnerability in older Core Lightning versions, and an update to Bitcoin Core's security policy. #### Alice & Bob researchers say a quantum attack on Bitcoin keys could be 17 times faster than expected

## Lightning & L2

#### Wavelength goes live on Bitcoin mainnet

Apps and AI agents can send and receive over Lightning with a few API calls: self-custodial, with no node, channels or liquidity to manage. Also floated: LayerTree, a non-custodial scaling design sharing one UTXO across up to a million users. [x.com](https://x.com/givelightning/status/2115137385323479389) [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2116295940319678601)
