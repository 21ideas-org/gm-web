## Regulation & Policy

#### Michael Saylor proposes rules letting banks lend against bitcoin

Saylor wants banks to custody bitcoin and lend against it, giving holders more ways to use their coins. He also calls for an insurance-industry path and a review of the capital rules applied to certain crypto exposures. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/michael-saylor-proposes-rules-letting-banks-lend-against-bitcoin/)

## Technology & Development

#### Early work on bitcoin-rs, an independent Bitcoin full node written in Rust

Like btcd, it is an independent implementation built to explore architectural and performance changes that are hard to pursue within Bitcoin Core. Script validation can run natively in Rust or through libbitcoinkernel, which also serves as an oracle to cross-check the native code. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bitcoin-rs-an-independent-bitcoin-full-node-written-in-rust/2918) [github.com](https://github.com/gosuda/bitcoin-rs)
