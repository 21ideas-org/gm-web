## Security & Privacy

#### Bitcoin Core merges a fix for a PSBT gap that could redirect funds without stealing keys

A SIGHASH\_SINGLE signing request with no matching output can leave recipients unbound, so funds could be redirected without any key theft. The PSBT repair is merged, but which release will ship it is not yet confirmed. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-cores-new-fix-closes-gap-that-could-redirect-funds-without-stealing-keys/)

## Regulation & Policy

#### Community bankers sue US banking regulator over 'side door' crypto charters

The Independent Community Bankers of America argues in federal court that the regulator overstepped its authority under the National Bank Act by granting national trust bank charters to crypto companies. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/community-bankers-sue-us-banking-regulator-over-side-door-crypto-charters/)

## Technology & Development

#### Proposal: descriptor syntax for hash preimages

jaonoctus proposes a private form such as sha256(preimage(HEX)), possibly as a new BIP extending BIP 379. Bitcoin Core's wallet could then import preimages like private keys. Today it needs an external tool to add them to a PSBT. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/descriptor-syntax-for-hash-preimages/2935#post_1) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-8720-summary-1)
