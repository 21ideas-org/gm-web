## Regulation & Policy

#### US Treasury orders MEXC cut off from the US financial system over alleged $16 billion in illicit flows

FinCEN's final order under section 9714(a) will bar US financial institutions from sending or receiving funds with MEXC Global 30 days after Federal Register publication. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/fincen-cuts-off-mexc-us-financial-system)

#### Rule that would allow crypto in 401(k)s reaches the White House for final review

The final Labor Department rule, which would create a fiduciary safe harbor for private equity, real estate and crypto in 401(k) menus, arrived at OIRA on Thursday, putting public release within reach. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/dol-rule-crypto-401k-white-house-review)

#### US Treasury and Fed drop proposed rule to track crypto transfers as small as $250

Withdrawing the 2020 proposal on international transfers keeps the existing $3,000 information-collection threshold while officials reassess the rules. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/us-treasury-fed-abandon-plan-track-smaller-bitcoin-crypto-transfers/)

#### Raspberry Pi kernel fix for TCP vulnerability is available for MyNode OS v0.3.52

[x.com](https://x.com/mynodebtc/status/2118519611012296755) [x.com](https://x.com/mynodebtc/status/2118519609720459360)

#### Connor Avila publishes draft BIPs for anti-exfil signing with BIP340 and ECDSA

Anti-exfil stops a compromised signing device leaking its seed through nonces. The drafts add three PSBT input fields and ship test vectors and reference code, but are unaudited, unreviewed by a cryptographer and not yet run on a device.
