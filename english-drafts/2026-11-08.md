## Regulation & Policy

#### Bank of Russia sets start date for its list of permitted cryptocurrencies

The rule limits non-qualified investors, almost all investors, to three assets and a ruble cap. Moscow Exchange plans a December 1 pilot of Bitcoin and Ethereum trading, with testing and a 300,000-ruble annual cap per intermediary for non-qualified buyers. [bits.media](https://bits.media/bank-rossii-nazval-datu-nachala-deystviya-spiska-razreshennykh-kriptovalyut/) [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/moscow-exchange-sets-dec-1-to-launch-bitcoin-and-ethereum-trading/)

#### White House reviews proposed SEC crypto broker-dealer rule after Democratic midterm win

The SEC sent the proposal to the White House on November 3. It targets four securities regulations on capital, custody and recordkeeping, as Democrats prepare to take control of both chambers of Congress. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/white-house-reviews-sec-crypto-rules-after-democratic-midterm-victory/)

#### EU sets up dedicated crypto task force to curb Russian sanctions evasion

The task force will sit within a new sanctions office coordinating how national authorities apply the rules, targeting networks such as A7, which provided a ruble stablecoin to skirt restrictions. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/eu-deploys-dedicated-crypto-task-force-to-block-russian-evasion/)

#### Indian enforcement agency raids CoinDCX over alleged routing of trades to Binance

India's Enforcement Directorate searched premises linked to CoinDCX and Delta Exchange India, alleging they routed users' trades to Binance, Bybit and other offshore platforms. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/india-ed-searches-coindcx-delta-exchange-binance/)

A tiny mining machine beat long odds in the first week of November. #### Bitcoin mining difficulty marks its longest stretch below a peak

Difficulty has spent 374 days below its October 2025 all-time high, beating the record set after the 2011 crash. Economist Saifedean Ammous flagged the milestone. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/bitcoin-mining-difficulty-longest-below-all-time-high/)

## Technology & Development

#### bitryonix proposes BIP395 for unspendable Taproot internal keys in wallet policies

The PR, now under discussion, adds an underscore placeholder so wallets and signers can rederive the key from the policy and derivation path without storing a random tweak. It includes a Python reference implementation and test vectors, which the author reports pass. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/unspendable-keys-in-descriptors/304?page=2#post_34) [github.com](https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/pull/2340)

#### Lightning liquidity purchases on Amboss's Magma up 66% year over year

Node operators bought 17.38 BTC of inbound liquidity on Magma in October, versus 10.48 BTC a year earlier, Amboss says. Across all orders, the average price has been 4.42% a year. [x.com](https://x.com/ambosstech/status/2119142864039096374) [amboss.tech](https://amboss.tech/magma)
