## Security & Privacy

#### Ledger says checks across Germany found no compromised wallets

Ledger said it inspected retail and distributor stock of its hardware wallets throughout Germany and found no compromised devices, after wallet owners there alleged on X that their devices had been tampered with. [bits.media](https://bits.media/ledger-otkazalas-priznat-vzlom-svoikh-koshelkov-v-evrope/) [x.com](https://x.com/Ledger/status/2110416915835338942)

#### Trezor to drop several resellers after Ledger's investigation into tampered wallets

Trezor plans to end work with several resellers from January 1, citing the findings of rival Ledger's investigation. A day earlier, Ledger acknowledged that an Asian partner had sold modified wallets with hidden spy modules. [bits.media](https://bits.media/trezor-sokratit-set-prodazh-iz-za-massovogo-vzloma-koshelkov-ledger/)

#### Bisq v2.1.14 fixes audit-found vulnerabilities; update required to keep trading

The security update moves to Tor v0.4.9.14, tightens Bisq Easy trade validation and network message limits, adds storage signatures, and stops APIs from returning private keys for existing identities. [github.com](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq2/releases/tag/v2.1.14) [x.com](https://x.com/bisq_network/status/2110560720660410767)

#### Wasabi Wallet v2.8.4 limits coinjoin payments to private funds and deprecates compatibility passphrase

Users who see the "Compatibility passphrase was used" warning at login should create a new wallet: a macOS clipboard bug five years ago silently truncated and corrupted pasted passphrases with non-ASCII characters. [github.com](https://github.com/WalletWasabi/WalletWasabi/releases/tag/v2.8.4) [x.com](https://x.com/wasabiwallet/status/2110050467052945910)
