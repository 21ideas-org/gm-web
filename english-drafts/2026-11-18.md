## Funds & ETFs

#### Wells Fargo more than doubles its Bitcoin ETF stake to $2.35 billion

Its third-quarter 13F shows 49.6 million IBIT shares worth $2.35 billion at Sept. 30, up from 24.4 million, while its Strategy stake fell about 33% to 1.34 million shares. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/wells-fargo-ibit-stake-doubles-strategy-cut)

#### Goldman Sachs more than doubles its Bitcoin ETF holdings to $1.28 billion

Its third-quarter 13F shows spot bitcoin ETF holdings at Sept. 30 up from about $580 million, led by a 52-fold jump in its position in Fidelity's fund, while its Strategy stake fell about 52%. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/goldman-sachs-bitcoin-etf-holdings)

#### BlackRock's IBIT ends Bitcoin ETFs' $1.9 billion exodus as seven rivals keep bleeding

IBIT was the only fund with positive flows on November 16, while seven peers recorded withdrawals. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/blackrock-single-handedly-ends-bitcoin-etfs-1-9-billion-exodus-as-seven-rivals-keep-bleeding/)

## Price & Market

## Technology & Development

#### Developer's STARK proof checks Bitcoin's UTXO set of 164 million coins at block 972,500

The proof verifies in about 0.5 s in a browser; 70.7 of the 72 s check is reading the 9.4 GB snapshot. It omits scripts, signatures, BIP68 relative locktimes and full sigop cost, so it is a bit weaker than assumevalid; external review is pending. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-developer-verifies-164-million-unspent-coins-in-just-72-seconds/) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/a-stark-proof-of-bitcoins-utxo-set-at-block-972-500-with-circuits-proven-in-lean/3070#post_4)
