## Security & Privacy

#### Blockstream publishes its Liquid Network security incident assessment

A flaw in Elements' rangeproof verification cache let a September 6 transaction validate without backing inputs, inflating the LBTC supply by roughly 4,000 unbacked LBTC, which the attacker moved out through Liquid's peg-out process. The investigation continues. [blog.blockstream.com](https://blog.blockstream.com/liquid-network-security-incident-assessment/) [sideswap.io](https://sideswap.io/?ref=blog.blockstream.com)

#### EU watchdogs warn quantum computers could pick crypto's locks

EU supervisors flag quantum computing as a future threat to blockchain security; Bitcoin developers are weighing a draft migration plan, while Ethereum targets 2029. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/eu-watchdogs-quantum-computers-crypto-locks)

## Regulation & Policy

#### Russia keeps its Bitcoin payment ban and advances the digital ruble

According to Tass, the digital ruble has been available for transactions since September 1, while bitcoin stays barred as a means of payment. [atlas21.com](https://atlas21.com/russia-keeps-ban-on-bitcoin-payments/)

#### Bank of Russia sets a deadline for illegal crypto exchangers to disappear

The central bank expects exchangers that refuse to obtain a permit to be gone after July 1 next year, when the government's crypto oversight law takes full effect, said the head of its financial monitoring and currency control department. [bits.media](https://bits.media/bank-rossii-nazval-sroki-ischeznoveniya-nelegalnykh-kriptoobmennikov/)

#### Bank of Russia allows mutual funds to invest in crypto instruments

The regulator changed the rules for unit investment funds (PIFs), adding new instruments including crypto — a regulated channel for Russian fund money. [bits.media](https://bits.media/bank-rossii-razreshil-paevym-investfondam-vkladyvatsya-v-kriptoinstrumenty/)

#### CFTC chair Selig says the regulator is preparing for '24-7, on-chain' markets

Mike Selig says the CFTC is looking forward to crypto and AI markets as the agency presses on without the Clarity Act. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/cftc-is-preparing-for-onchain-markets)
