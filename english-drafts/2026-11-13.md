## Technology & Development

#### Draft BIP proposes a post-quantum bc1z address format using ML-DSA-65

Hans Stringuer's draft, developed within the safe-btc project, would commit to a SHA-256 hash of the ML-DSA public key, revealed only at spend time; a spend would carry a 3,309-byte signature and a 1,952-byte key. #### Draft BIP proposes a common message encoding for external signing devices

Pyth's draft, which grew out of a Delving Bitcoin thread on QR-based signing, is transport-agnostic and covers descriptors, miniscript, BIP-388 wallet policies, MuSig2 and silent payment sends. Feedback is requested. #### Discussion of decentralized coinbase payout protocols draws on P2Pool's history

Data shared in the DCPP thread show a typical P2Pool block paid about 200 addresses in 2012 and 390 in 2014, peaking at 553; a participant estimates Bitmain hardware handles about 25 coinbase outputs or fewer. #### A proposed K-of-N custody design for Lightning nodes without a spec change

Tony's design combines threshold ECDSA signing with threshold encryption of offline-precomputed revocation secrets, so that no single online machine can compromise channel funds. ## Lightning & L2

#### Open-source pi402 stack lets AI agents buy inference with bitcoin over Lightning

The stack pairs a Pi extension for L402+MPP negotiation with the lightmeter metering daemon and the Aperture reverse proxy; roasbeef's live endpoint sells inference at 1 sat per 500 tokens. [x.com](https://x.com/roasbeef/status/2120983638183752053) [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2120984067290325367)

#### lnd v0.21.8 and v0.20.8 released

The releases bring bug fixes, P2P stability improvements and on-chain sweeping improvements; all users are recommended to update. [x.com](https://x.com/roasbeef/status/2120677656673075325) [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2120678378135285966)

#### Amboss says Bayesian Binary Search makes Lightning balance probing 3 to 6% more efficient

Instead of splitting at the midpoint, the method splits where a channel's hidden balance is likely to sit. The peer-reviewed paper, written with Stillmark, appeared in Algorithms (MDPI, 2025); the method runs in production at Amboss. [x.com](https://x.com/ambosstech/status/2120954804394676342) [amboss.tech](https://amboss.tech/blog/bayesian-binary-search)
