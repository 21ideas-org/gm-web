## Security & Privacy

#### Ledger says a criminal group secretly tampered with hardware wallets in a $90 million theft

According to Ledger, the group operated in Southeast Asia and was behind the theft of $90 million in crypto from hardware wallets. The company classified the incident as an attack on a local distribution channel. [bits.media](https://bits.media/ledger-rasskazala-o-tayno-vskryvavshey-apparatnye-koshelki-gruppe-prestupnikov/)

#### Bunker mode and bitcoin address reuse: what bitcoiners should know

Ethereum Foundation researcher Justin Drake urged moving funds to addresses never spent from, citing a possible AI-driven threat to elliptic-curve signatures. Past address reuse alone does not mean keys are compromised. [blog.casa.io](https://blog.casa.io/bunker-mode-bitcoin-address-reuse/) [x.com](https://x.com/drakefjustin/status/2107837081313505768)

## Lightning & L2

#### LayerTree announced as a non-custodial bitcoin scaling solution that connects to Lightning

According to its announcement, LayerTree uses covenants to let one UTXO be shared by a million users, each able to exit in a single transaction; users need not be online to receive, and funds never time out. [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2110812651882217677) [x.com](https://x.com/roasbeef/status/2110813488822239619)

#### Eclair v0.14.4 released with security hardening fixes

ACINQ highly recommends upgrading, as some of the fixed issues, mostly found by project Loupe, can be exploited by malicious nodes. The patch is compatible with previous versions, and channels need not be closed. [github.com](https://github.com/ACINQ/eclair/releases/tag/v0.14.4)

#### Post proposes a protocol for Silent Payments from multiparty wallets

BIP-352 does not recommend FROST- or MuSig2-based inputs due to the lack of a security proof. The proposal has the wallet compute an ECDH share as a weighted sum of signers' contributions, aggregated into a single BIP-374 proof. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/silent-payments-for-multiparty-wallets/2960) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-8776-a-shared-interface-for-multiparty-keys-1)
