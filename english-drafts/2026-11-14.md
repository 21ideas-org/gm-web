## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin Optech #431: three vulnerabilities in older Bitcoin Core versions disclosed

The newsletter also links draft BIPs for exfiltration-resistant signing, announces a plan to mine the testnet5 genesis block and describes an investigation into degraded compact block reconstruction. [bitcoinops.org](https://bitcoinops.org/en/newsletters/2026/11/13/)

#### Zaenus revises draft BIP for post-quantum addresses using ML-DSA-65

Version 0.2 adds a key-type byte for future schemes, a BIP-341-based sighash, full spending rules and test vectors. It fixes a bech32m checksum bug and withdraws a SNARK aggregation prototype. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/bip-draft-post-quantum-address-format-bc1z-using-ml-dsa-65/3067#post_2) [github.com](https://github.com/Zaenus/btc-safe/blob/4e09003f69ee276f8df1eadcc913ab98a84dbf52/docs/bip/0001-pq-address-format.md)

#### Liam Gilligan posts draft BIP for P2P transport feature negotiation

The proposal would repurpose the version packet BIP-324 left unspecified so nodes can negotiate transport features, a framework for encryption upgrades and P2P authentication. [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/1b45f614-6251-4c79-8297-f880edc07c8bn@googlegroups.com/) [mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz](https://mailing-list.bitcoindevs.xyz/bitcoindev/1b45f614-6251-4c79-8297-f880edc07c8bn@googlegroups.com/1.1-a.txt)

#### Side project produces a STARK proof of Bitcoin's UTXO set at block 972,500

l-henri's 1.6 MB proof, verifiable in a browser in about half a second, covers the header chain from genesis and replays blocks 935,001–972,500; its UTXO set hash matches Bitcoin Core's. Scripts and signatures are not checked. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/a-stark-proof-of-bitcoins-utxo-set-at-block-972-500-with-circuits-proven-in-lean/3070) [starknet-innovation.github.io](https://starknet-innovation.github.io/starked-bitcoin/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Strike introduces Trusted Addresses that bypass send limits for your own wallets

After a five-day waiting period with notifications and an option to cancel, sends to a saved address such as cold storage skip the send limit; limits stay in place for every other address. [x.com](https://x.com/Strike/status/2121290851506700702)

#### @n1ckler tests post-quantum SHRINCS signatures on current hardware wallets

On devices including Blockstream Jade, hash-based signing took from a few seconds to a minute and a half, suggesting that in principle no new hardware is needed. [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2121293645559074960)
