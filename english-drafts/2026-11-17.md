## Regulation & Policy

#### Supreme Court ends Custodia Bank's fight for a Fed master account

The Court denied Custodia's petition in No. 26-62 on Monday, leaving in place a Tenth Circuit ruling that leaves Federal Reserve Banks free to deny crypto banks master accounts, even when they are eligible. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/supreme-court-ends-custodia-fed-master)

#### German Bundestag approves bill letting customs freeze crypto over €100,000 without a criminal case

The bill would let German customs freeze crypto and other assets of unclear origin worth over €100,000. It now goes to the Bundesrat. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/german-bundestag-crypto-over-100000)

#### California voters bar new taxes on holding personal assets, including crypto

The state amendment, approved in a statewide vote, prohibits new taxes on owning personal assets such as crypto, stocks, retirement savings and business stakes. [bits.media](https://bits.media/v-kalifornii-otkazalis-ot-naloga-na-vladenie-kriptovalyutoy/)

#### Senate Agriculture Committee to hear Trump's CFTC nominees on Tuesday

Nathan Anonick and Matthew Rowland, President Trump's bipartisan picks, would join CFTC Chairman Michael Selig, currently the agency's only sitting commissioner. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/cftc-nominees-senate-hearing-tuesday)
