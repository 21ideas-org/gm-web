## Regulation & Policy

#### US Treasury drops its mixer rule as prosecutors push to outlaw mixers

FinCEN scrapped proposed rules on unhosted wallets and mixing, but the same day prosecutors invoked a new Bitcoin Fog appeal precedent in the Tornado Cash case, a move that could criminalize the use of financial privacy tools. [therage.co](https://www.therage.co/mixer-rule-sterlingov-tornado-cash/) [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/treasury-drops-crypto-surveillance)

#### Bank of Russia grants first permits to crypto exchange services

The regulator authorized the first four crypto exchange services and the first five digital depositories. [bits.media](https://bits.media/bank-rossii-vydal-pervye-razresheniya-kriptoobmennikam/)

## Security & Privacy

#### BTCPay Server 2.4.5 blocks private-network outbound requests by default

To prevent SSRF, Lightning connections, LNURL requests, invoice notifications and webhooks can no longer reach private networks unless operators allow them. The release also tightens refund approvals and hides old invoice data from public endpoints. [github.com](https://github.com/btcpayserver/btcpayserver/releases/tag/v2.4.5)

#### Fedimint v0.12.2 fixes a gateway bug that could lose funds

A bug in LNv2 payment handling could cause v0.12 Lightning gateways to lose funds; operators should upgrade as soon as possible. Details are withheld for now. Guardians, wallet users and v0.11 gateways are not affected. [github.com](https://github.com/fedimint/fedimint/releases/tag/v0.12.2)

#### Alby Hub v1.24.1 brings UX improvements and security updates

The patch refreshes payment status components and QR codes, adds transaction list filtering, supports migrating from Postgres to SQLite, and includes dependency updates and bug fixes. [github.com](https://github.com/getAlby/hub/releases/tag/v1.24.1)

## Institutions & Treasuries

#### Strive makes its largest bitcoin purchase in four months

The firm co-founded by Vivek Ramaswamy bought 2,000 BTC last week for $169 million at an average of $84,422 per coin, its biggest buy since late May to early June. [bits.media](https://bits.media/strive-sovershila-krupneyshuyu-za-chetyre-mesyatsa-pokupku-bitkoinov/)

#### Bitcoin rally delivers $4.1 billion tax windfall for Strategy

The figure comes from Strategy's October filing and reflects an accounting consequence of the bitcoin rally. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-rally-delivers-4-1-billion-tax-windfall-for-strategy/)

#### US government moves $71 million in bitcoin after six weeks of silence

Government-linked wallets shifted 833.6 BTC, their first movement since Aug. 26. The coins trace back to the Potapenko-Turogin fraud case and the 2016 Bitfinex hack. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/us-government-moves-71m-bitcoin-stash-after-6-week-silence/)

#### CEO of $350M St. Cloud on being the first credit union to put bitcoin on its core ledger

#### Antoine Riard maps the path to finalizing BIP54

BIP54, the consensus cleanup, would fix the timewarp attack, slow validation of pre-segwit scripts, 64-byte transaction merkle malleability and duplicate coinbases. The 64-byte rule is still the most contested; Riard outlines his view of the remaining steps.
