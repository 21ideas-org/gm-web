## Security & Privacy

#### Binance says hackers posed as employers and lured staff into fake job interviews

According to Binance's official account of the $6.3 billion hack, the attackers exploited no technical vulnerability. Instead, they deceived exchange employees by posing as employers and staging fake job interviews. [bits.media](https://bits.media/khakery-prikinulis-rabotodatelyami-i-pozvali-sotrudnika-binance-na-sobesedovanie/)

#### BTCPay urges Bitcoin merchants to replace wallets after hackers steal an estimated $1.6 million

The November 2 update links the hot-wallet thefts to a flaw patched in August. It warns that updating cannot remove access attackers have already gained. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/btcpay-urges-bitcoin-merchants-to-replace-wallets-after-hackers-steal-an-estimated-1-6-million/)

## Regulation & Policy

#### Moscow Exchange plans to launch Bitcoin, Ether and USDT trading in December

Trading is planned to start on December 1 under Russia's new law. Non-qualified investors will face annual purchase limits. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/moscow-exchange-bitcoin-ether-usdt-trading-december)

#### Bank of Russia: crypto exchangers may hold assets outside digital depositories

In clarifications to recent legislative changes, the central bank said exchangers can keep assets in wallets, so-called "identifier addresses", that are not tied to digital depositories. [bits.media](https://bits.media/bank-rossii-kriptoobmennikam-mozhno-khranit-aktivy-vne-tsifrovykh-depozitariev/)

#### TD Cowen: a Democratic Congress would sink the CLARITY Act for two years

TD Cowen's Jaret Seiberg says a Democratic sweep of Congress would kill the bill for two years, hand Elizabeth Warren the Banking Committee and let Democrats block Trump's picks for the SEC and CFTC. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/td-cowen-democratic-congress-clarity-act)

## Price & Market

#### Bitcoin rebounds from $76K amid sizable ETF outflows and liquidations

Bitcoin fell to $76,268 on Tuesday before recovering above $77,700. Investors weighed the fallout from the Binance hack and uncertainty around the US midterm elections. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/market-updates/bitcoin-price-rebounds-76k-sizable-etf-outflows-liquidations/)

#### Dormant bitcoin awakenings cool in October as transfers plunge 81% from August

Dormant wallets dating back to 2012 moved 2,159.15 BTC, worth $167.56 million, last month, a second consecutive monthly slowdown. Wallets from 2013 dominated, while addresses from Bitcoin's earliest years remained conspicuously absent. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/octobers-dormant-bitcoin-awakenings-cool-transfers-plunge-august/)

#### LDK fix blocks a probe linking private Lightning channels to their onchain funding

LDK's October 25 fix rejects a funding-location lookup used by the probe, which requires connections to both endpoints of an unannounced channel. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-lightning-fix-blocks-a-probe-linking-private-channels-to-onchain-funding/)

#### Liana v16.0 adds French, Italian and Portuguese and enables Taproot by default in the installer

Encrypted descriptor backups move to BIP138, which v15 and older can't read. The database migration is one-way, so wallets opened in v16 won't open in v15 or below. The release also fixes an RBF attack by malicious Electrum servers. It was released because 0.22.0 missed a version bump. The release includes mint database migrations, so operators should back up databases and stop older mint workers before upgrading.
