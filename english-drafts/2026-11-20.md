## Technology & Development

#### Draft security analysis published for SHRINCS, a proposed Bitcoin post-quantum signature scheme

The hash-based scheme was specified by Blockstream researchers and collaborators. Concerns remain over wallet safety, transaction costs and implementation complexity; Dan Boneh, Charles Guillemet and Yehuda Lindell have flagged tradeoffs. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/technology/bitcoin-quantum-defense-change-value-proposition-btc-researcher/) [x.com](https://x.com/MARAFoundation_/status/2123447558747951461)

#### Testnet5 launches, letting miners trial four proposed fixes

Mining pools can test the blocks their software builds, while Bitcoin Core and Sparrow Wallet prepare support. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-launches-testnet5-and-gives-miners-a-real-trial-of-four-proposed-fixes/)

#### Draft BIP proposes NestedMuSig2, a recursive extension of MuSig2

Beulah Evanjalin's proposal extends BIP327 to hierarchical signing groups, letting a tree of signers produce one BIP340-compatible signature without revealing its internal structure. Feedback and reviews are requested. #### Murch revives call to add BIP Editors as review backlog grows

Murch says editors keep falling behind on submissions and prefers a team of three regularly active editors over dropping full reads of new BIPs; volunteers could commit to 12–24-month terms. ## Security & Privacy

#### BitBox Cresta update fixes firmware and app security issues, adds new features

Fixes include a bootloader flaw that could weaken firmware downgrade protection. BitBox reports no sign of exploitation and urges updating; devices on firmware v9.17.0 or older must first install v9.17.1. [blog.bitbox.swiss](https://blog.bitbox.swiss/en/bitbox-11-2026-cresta-update/) [bitbox.swiss](https://bitbox.swiss/download?source=update)

#### Ledger expands wallet tampering probe to Berlin and Dubai

Physically altered devices from the incident disclosed in October surfaced beyond Southeast Asia. Ledger found the modifications were designed to capture recovery phrases during setup and is offering factory inspections. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/ledger-expands-crypto-wallet-tampering-probe-to-berlin-and-dubai/)

#### Trezor Suite v26.11.2 warns before sending to new addresses

The warning is meant to catch addresses copied from phishing transactions. Suite also adds checks during device setup to help verify packaging and confirm the device hasn't been tampered with. [github.com](https://github.com/trezor/trezor-suite/releases/tag/v26.11.2)
