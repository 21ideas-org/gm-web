## Security & Privacy

#### Core Lightning warns attackers are targeting unpatched nodes

Node operators running version 26.06.7 or earlier were told to upgrade immediately. [cointelegraph.com](https://cointelegraph.com/news/core-lightning-urges-upgrade-amid-reports-attackers-targeting-unpatched-nodes)

#### Disclosure: two denial-of-service vulnerabilities fixed in Eclair v0.14.1

Eclair v0.14.0 and earlier had a channel-opening race that leaked about 1 MB of memory per second until the node crashed, plus a rate-limiter bypass enabling unfunded channel floods. Matt Morehouse found the race while fuzzing; users should upgrade to v0.14.1. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/disclosure-dos-vulnerabilities-fixed-in-eclair-v0-14-1/2928) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-8705-lnf-2026-0003-open_channel-race-dos-1)

## Regulation & Policy

#### SEC proposes letting advisers and funds self-custody crypto assets

The proposal would update long-standing safekeeping requirements for investment advisers and regulated funds under federal securities law, allowing them to hold crypto assets directly. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/regulation-and-legal/sec-proposes-letting-advisers-and-funds-self-custody-crypto-assets/)

#### Senate tax bill would free stablecoin spending while Bitcoin stays on IRS forms

The circulated proposal gives eligible dollar-stablecoin purchases no-gain/no-loss treatment with no stated value cap, while ordinary Bitcoin spending still requires disposition reporting. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/senate-tax-bill-frees-stablecoin-spending-while-bitcoin-stays-on-irs-forms/)

#### US Treasury announces "unprecedented measures" against A7

Treasury labeled the Russia-linked crypto project A7 a shadow banking structure used by Iran to evade sanctions; OFAC also designated it a significant transnational criminal organization. [bits.media](https://bits.media/minfin-ssha-obyavil-o-bespretsedentnykh-merakh-protiv-a7/)

#### Binance suspected of serving EU customers without a license

ESMA and regulators in France, Germany and Greece are examining whether the largest exchange by trading volume used the "reverse solicitation" loophole to keep operating in the EU without a license. [bits.media](https://bits.media/binance-zapodozrili-v-obsluzhivanii-klientov-iz-evrosoyuza-bez-litsenzii/)
