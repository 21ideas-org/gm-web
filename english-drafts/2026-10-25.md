#### Bitcoin sinks to $79,146 as liquidations rock weekend trading

## Security & Privacy

#### Electrum 4.8.3 fixes flaw letting malicious servers trick users into paying attackers

A malicious server could swap a just-broadcast transaction for a fake one, so fee-bumping it via RBF pays the attacker. Electrum says servers are exploiting this in the wild; one user lost funds. Those using their own trusted servers are not affected. [github.com](https://github.com/spesmilo/electrum/security/advisories/GHSA-6jp4-8v48-cc5v) [x.com](https://x.com/ElectrumWallet/status/2113935057656369564)

#### Keystone discloses data breach exposing customers' shipping details

Names, contact details, addresses and order details were exposed; Keystone says recovery phrases, private keys and payment data were not. It warns of scammers posing as the company and urges users never to share a recovery phrase. [x.com](https://x.com/KeystoneWallet/status/2114054080754770137) [keyst.one](http://keyst.one/)
