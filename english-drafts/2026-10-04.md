## Technology & Development

#### Gregory Sanders: compact block reconstructions now need far more round trips, mostly over Rune mint chains

Per a bot-assisted analysis of his node, blocks needing no round trip fell from 45–55% in early July to mostly 2–15% since Jul 30; the missing transactions are almost all Rune mint chains. Anthony Towns disputes that relay is broken, still seeing mostly sub-second reconstructions. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/stats-on-compact-block-reconstructions/1052?page=3#post_56) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/stats-on-compact-block-reconstructions/1052?page=3#post_54)

## Security & Privacy

#### COLDCARD: firmware update fixes seed generation but doesn't repair existing seeds

Seeds created on affected firmware stay affected after updating. COLDCARD says they require a new seed and an on-chain migration of funds; a clone or restore won't help. [x.com](https://x.com/COLDCARDwallet/status/2106383840176419079) [coldcard.com](https://coldcard.com/security/migrate)

## Lightning & L2

#### Lightning Labs presents Wavelength, its Ark implementation

According to a report from the talk by @dario\_nakamoto, Lightning Labs plans to use Taproot Assets for stablecoins on its Ark and uses the same "free window" of refreshes as Bark. [x.com](https://x.com/matthewvuk2/status/2105993909377937627) [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2106096484932272379)

#### Calle unveils Federated Cashu at btc++

Users needn't trust the operator with their privacy but still trust it with their security. Any 4 of 5 operators keep the federation running, and a new blind BLS signature scheme is involved. [x.com](https://x.com/matthewvuk2/status/2105601950582702317) [x.com](https://x.com/CashuBTC/status/2105625578996895755)
