## Lightning & L2

#### Wavelength launches on mainnet with a self-custodial bitcoin payments SDK

The Wavelength SDK lets humans and agents send and receive bitcoin payments with a few API calls, self-custodial from the first command and with no node or liquidity to manage. [x.com](https://x.com/lightning/status/2113637153070542859) [lightning.engineering](https://lightning.engineering/posts/2026-10-23-wavelength-mainnet-launch/)

## Security & Privacy

#### Bitcoin Core developers revise vulnerability disclosure policy

Developer Antoine Poinsot told peers on the mailing list that the approach was revised because large language models have made finding software bugs easier. [bits.media](https://bits.media/razrabotchiki-bitcoin-core-izmenili-politiku-raskrytiya-uyazvimostey/)

#### StartOS 0.4.0.3 strengthens network privacy and access controls

DNS queries now follow the chosen outbound gateway, login rate limits and service-access checks are tightened, and failed service updates no longer restore stale data from an earlier update. [github.com](https://github.com/Start9Labs/start-technologies/releases/tag/start-os/v0.4.0.3)

#### Bisq 1.10.10 security update ships the latest Tor security release

The update bundles Tor 0.4.9.14 via netlayer v0.7.12, renews the release signing keys, removes the unused expired key 4A133008 and adds DAO state checkpoints at heights 970000 and 971000. [github.com](https://github.com/bisq-network/bisq/releases/tag/v1.10.10)

#### US spot Bitcoin ETFs shed $635 million as BTC tests their holdings’ estimated $81,500 cost basis

The Oct. 22 net outflows were the funds’ largest daily outflow since June 25 and reversed nearly 60% of earlier inflows. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-etf-outflows-holdings-cost-basis/)

#### Franklin Templeton clears final hurdle for ETFs that turn stock dividends into bitcoin

The $1.7 trillion asset manager registered two Nasdaq-listed funds that launch with a 95/5 split between US stocks and bitcoin, with every dividend buying more bitcoin. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/franklin-templeton-clears-final-hurdle)

## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin Optech Newsletter #428 covers a single-issuer layer 2 with onchain exit

It describes a design whose users can redeem their bitcoin onchain without the issuer’s cooperation and summarizes a proposal to include hash preimages in output script descriptors. [bitcoinops.org](https://bitcoinops.org/en/newsletters/2026/10/23/)

#### bitKingAE proposes ‘proof-of-decay’ for unmigrated quantum-exposed UTXOs

Under the idea, exposed UTXOs not moved to post-quantum keys by a multi-year deadline would gradually lose part of their balance, e.g. every 50,000 blocks, as an alternative to a hard freeze or doing nothing. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/a-novel-post-quantum-approach-proof-of-decay-cryptographic-half-life-for-unmigrated-dormant-utxos/2970) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/a-novel-post-quantum-approach-proof-of-decay-cryptographic-half-life-for-unmigrated-dormant-utxos/2970#post_2)

#### Simplicity pitched as Bitcoin’s last soft fork

On stage at @LuganoPlanB, @adam3us argued that one expressive language with formal semantics could raise the security bar for Bitcoin scripts and open the door to opt-in Confidential Transactions or covenants. [x.com](https://x.com/Blockstream/status/2113566802479624441)
