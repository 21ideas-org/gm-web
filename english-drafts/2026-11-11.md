## Security & Privacy

#### Bitcoin Core discloses three vulnerabilities, one rated high severity

Fixed in Bitcoin Core 29.2 and later 29.x releases and in 30.0 onward: a reorg mempool bug that can deny miners valid block templates, a Tor whitelist flaw enabling DoS or state leaks, and a misplaced witness check letting peers stall block processing. #### LNbits ships urgent 1.7.0 security update after attacks reportedly drain funds

Per Bitcoin Magazine, active attacks began November 8. LNbits urges all users to update immediately using its upgrade guidance; operators must upgrade before restarting servers, as earlier versions put funds at risk. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/lnbits-releases-1-7-0-urgent-security-update-after-november-8-attacks-drain-funds) [x.com](https://x.com/lnbits/status/2120254437419622696)

#### Binance warns an iOS flaw could let malicious apps steal wallet seed phrases

Binance says a public iOS sandbox escape dubbed worse\_query, affecting three iOS versions, could let malicious apps read seed phrases, private keys and login data stored by other apps. ## Institutions & Treasuries

#### MARA sells 13,597 BTC for $1.1 billion and says more sales are coming

The miner sold about 40% of its stack after Sept. 30, used $750 million to repay all its BTC-backed term loans, and plans further sales to fund AI infrastructure, convertible note puts and possible buybacks. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/mara-sells-13597-bitcoin-1-1-billion) [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/mara-dumps-bitcoin-to-erase-a-750m-collateral-risk/)

#### Trump Media pledges 73% of its bitcoin to an options program as $1 billion note deadline nears

Its Q3 10-Q shows 11,385 of roughly 15,649 BTC pledged to an options program whose counterparty can reuse the coins, with nearly all the rest backing $1 billion of notes. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/trump-media-pledges-73-percent-bitcoin)

#### Hyperscale Data sells its last bitcoin to fund an AI data center

It sold its remaining 211.56 BTC for about $16 million by Nov. 8, bringing sales since late July to about 1,113 BTC, months after chasing a $100 million treasury, as it races to energize 10 MW of AI capacity. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/hyperscale-data-sells-last-bitcoin-ai)

## Regulation & Policy

#### Poland's Sejm votes 240-202 to let officials freeze bitcoin before court review

The sanctions bill, passed by the lower house on Friday, would let the interior minister freeze bitcoin and bank funds of people on a national list; a court appeal would not pause the freeze. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/poland-votes-240-202-freeze-crypto)

#### Texas bill would let cities invest up to $10 million in bitcoin

Rep. Ron Reynolds filed House Bill 815 on Monday, which would allow each Texas city or county to put up to $10 million of public money into bitcoin starting Sept. 1, 2027. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/texas-bill-invest-10-million-bitcoin)

#### Miami-Dade County now accepts bitcoin for property taxes

Tax Collector Dariel Fernandez said Monday his office accepts bitcoin online and in person; every payment is converted to dollars before it reaches the county. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/miami-dade-takes-bitcoin-property-taxes)

#### Binance halts transfers involving MEXC after US sanctions

The exchange said it is stopping transactions involving MEXC and related entities, a day after MEXC was placed under US sanctions. [bits.media](https://bits.media/binance-prekrashchaet-obrabotku-perevodov-s-uchastiem-birzhi-mexc/)

## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin developers merge three new BIPs that could lower transaction fees

Per CryptoSlate's modeling, full aggregation could cut fees about 14% for a joint payment or 27% for consolidation; activation and wallet support remain unresolved. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoin-devs-merge-3-new-bips-and-transaction-fees-could-plummet/)

#### Anthony Towns proposes launching testnet5 at the end of the week

He proposes mining the genesis block aligned with the mainnet retarget at block 975,744, with a 0%-fee solo-mining pool; the genesis block is tweaked to ease pooled mining and uses an unspendable OP\_RETURN output.
