## Security & Privacy

#### LNbits tells operators to take servers offline after a wave of attacks

LNbits said security patches are coming and told operators to stop their servers until then. It also flagged a potential bug in the Paywall extension and, as a precaution, disabled its hosted instances, saying service would resume within 24 hours. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/security/lightning-network-security-scare-forces-lnbits-servers-offline/) [x.com](https://x.com/lnbits/status/2119408009700937798)

#### Memory-exhaustion DoS disclosed in Eclair 0.14.0, fixed in 0.14.1

Any peer that can connect to an Eclair 0.14.0 node, even without a channel, can make it buffer unlimited commitment\_signed messages via start\_batch until it runs out of memory. Users should upgrade to 0.14.1 or later. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/disclosure-memory-exhaustion-dos-in-eclair-0-14-0/3057) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/#p-9002-memory-exhaustion-dos-via-start_batch-in-eclair-0140-1)

#### OKX reportedly flags MEXC-linked deposits after US Treasury blacklisting

Social media reports say some exchanges, including OKX, have begun flagging deposits from MEXC-linked addresses. ## Technology & Development

#### Bitcoin block packs 11,255 transactions, nearing the all-time record

The block on Saturday, Nov. 7, 2026, was the second-highest by transaction count in network history, close to a record set in 2015. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/featured/bitcoin-packs-11255-transactions-into-a-single-block-nears-record/)

#### @lukecarriere publishes v2.1 of the co-signer spending policies BIP draft

The revision adds strict verifier semantics, versioned commitments and signed finality and fixes the k-of-n threshold. The full schema, exact-byte vectors and reference implementation remain unfinished; the author is seeking feedback on serialization options. ## Lightning & L2

#### Average public Lightning channel roughly doubles to 0.097 BTC since 2022

Amboss data show 0.048 BTC in June 2022 vs 0.097 BTC on November 1, 2026: fewer public channels, but larger ones that can carry bigger payments along a single route. The figure is public capacity divided by active channels. [x.com](https://x.com/ambosstech/status/2119505260305891801) [amboss.space](https://amboss.space/stats)
