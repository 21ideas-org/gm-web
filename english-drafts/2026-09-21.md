## Technology & Development

#### Sparrow Wallet runs on a $40 handheld gaming console

DesobedienteTec, who previously ported SeedSigner to a gaming handheld, built this as a fully functional proof of concept with two microSD slots, camera support and a virtual keyboard. The same developer also maintains SeedSignerOS, a hardened, RAM-only, reproducible Linux system. [x.com](https://x.com/BitcoinNewsCom/status/2101427067568914691) [x.com](https://x.com/sesi_the_man/status/2101674548806508845) [x.com](https://x.com/DesobedienteTec/status/2101406226672165309/video/1) [x.com](https://x.com/sesi_the_man/status/2101675814051824066) [twitter.com](https://twitter.com/bitcoinnewscom/status/2101427067568914691)

#### Developer floats elliptic-curve one-time signatures for onchain attestation

AdamISZ asks whether swapping the hash function in Lamport/Winternitz-style one-time signatures for EC scalar multiplication would let Script verify a FROST threshold attestation, which hash-based constructions cannot do at scale. This is an exploratory discussion post, not a proposal or implementation. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/ec-ots-onchain-has-anyone-considered-it/2901)

## Security & Privacy

#### Why keeping your private keys safe won't always stop theft

Almost 4,000 BTC left Liquid's reserve on Sept. 6 through a withdrawal the network itself approved, with no key compromise involved — the software accepted a withdrawal that should never have qualified. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/who-pays-when-bitcoin-disappears-without-anyone-stealing-the-keys/)

#### Malicious iOS app read wallet data from other apps on the same phone

SlowMist and OKX confirmed that FomoPepe versions 1.1-1.2 carried a kernel exploit that escaped the app sandbox and read the Keychain directly, meaning a phone-based wallet depends on every other app and unpatched flaw on the device. The account reporting this sells hardware wallets. [x.com](https://x.com/KeystoneWallet/status/2101642535424630979) [twitter.com](https://twitter.com/SlowMist_Team/status/2101211432541192615)
