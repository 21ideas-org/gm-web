## Security & Privacy

#### Core Lightning v26.06.9 fixes security vulnerabilities and a gossip regression

Security fixes span channel reestablishment, splicing, HTLC handling during shutdown, onion handling and more; the 26.06.8 gossip throttle regression is fixed. Tests for the fixes are temporarily withheld. Developers strongly recommend upgrading. [github.com](https://github.com/ElementsProject/lightning/releases/tag/v26.06.9) [x.com](https://x.com/Core_LN/status/2107825919465308463)

#### Europol: quantum threat targets exposed wallet keys more than blockchains

In a report published Wednesday, the agency pushes back on quantum doomsday scenarios, warning that vulnerable keys cannot be retroactively secured. [news.bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/technology/europol-warns-quantum-threat-could-put-crypto-fortunes-risk/)

#### Bitcoin Magazine breaks down Bitcoin's quantum exposure at block 950,000

A piece from The Quantum Issue examines how much of Bitcoin's supply is vulnerable to quantum attack and the logistical questions of changing that. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/print/the-quantum-issue-bitcoin-quantum-exposure-at-block-950000)
