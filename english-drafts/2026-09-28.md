## Technology & Development

#### New Bitcoin proposal could rescue locked multisig wallets, at a hidden cost

The proposal's descriptor backup could aid multisig recovery, but a server that already knows an eligible xpub may be able to see the encrypted backup file's metadata. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/bitcoins-bip138-draft-shows-why-a-seed-may-not-rescue-a-multisig-wallet/)

#### How encrypted mempools work

A public mempool is open data anyone can analyze, and others can exploit that transparency in bad faith. Encrypted mempools are one proposed answer to the problem. [bits.media](https://bits.media/kogda-yavnoe-stanovitsya-taynym-kak-rabotayut-zashifrovannye-mempuly/)

#### 0xB10C: compact block relay is "essentially broken right now"

Updated stats from 0xB10C show that since mid-July only about 1–5% of compact blocks reconstruct without requesting transactions, with nodes fetching roughly 100 kB of transactions per block on average. [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/t/stats-on-compact-block-reconstructions/1052?page=3#post_53) [delvingbitcoin.org](https://delvingbitcoin.org/uploads/default/original/2X/2/2466fdf197f7f013d05e2da0343d5bab204d8171.jpeg)

## Security & Privacy

#### Core Lightning patches a flaw that could let revoked channel state escape penalty

The fix shipped in v26.06.7; operators on older builds and some early Docker images should check whether they need to upgrade. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/core-lightning-revoked-channel-penalty-flaw/)

#### Coruna/DarkSword exploit chain used in the wild to drain wallets from iPhones, researcher warns

Per P3b7\_ (reposted by Ledger), a single Safari click chains WebKit memory corruption, a PAC bypass and a sandbox escape to root, then pulls Keychain and wallet data to steal seeds. The advice: update iOS and move seeds to a hardware wallet. [x.com](https://x.com/P3b7_/status/2102013118209503688) [x.com](https://x.com/Ledger/status/2102028546348609592)

#### Reported COLDCARD seed-entropy thefts total 1,830 BTC

According to a post by intangiblecoins, the BTC was taken in three waves plus more than 30 smaller incidents; 256 victims have come forward to Galaxy Research, with a median loss of 1.1 BTC. The attackers have not yet been found. [x.com](https://x.com/intangiblecoins/status/2104165550830100752) [x.com](https://x.com/sesi_the_man/status/2104194978675355763)
