## Regulation & Policy

#### UK's 2027 crypto rules let firms remove trust protection from Bitcoin lent for yield

With FCA applications now open, the regime expected in October 2027 keeps safeguards for in-scope retail borrowing collateral but grants a lending trust exemption. Bitcoin lent out for yield could lose that protection. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/uks-2027-crypto-rules-let-firms-remove-trust-protection-from-bitcoin-lent-for-yield/)

#### Dutch government to introduce a capital gains tax from 2028

The Netherlands plans to levy capital gains on major assets starting in 2028, raising the question of whether bitcoin gains will be taxed. [bitcoinmagazine.com](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/dutch-government-introduce-capital-gains)

## Security & Privacy

#### BIP461 proposal aims to expose hidden key leaks in Bitcoin signatures

The proposal would make compliant ECDSA signatures reproducible, so comparing them can flag deviations. A deviation alone would not prove that firmware is malicious. [cryptoslate.com](https://cryptoslate.com/new-bitcoin-upgrade-catches-hidden-key-leaks-hiding-the-exact-fix/)

#### UK facial-scanning trial falls flat

The test scanned more than half a million faces and produced one false positive. [atlas21.com](https://atlas21.com/uk-rail-facial-recognition-pilot-produces-no-matches/)

#### Liquid Network begins independent security audit as it works to restore peg-outs

The external audit covers Elements v23.3.4. The Liquid Federation is also updating its PAK list, replacing existing entries and ensuring all peg-out keys are secured in cold storage. It promises further updates on resuming peg-outs shortly. [x.com](https://x.com/Liquid_BTC/status/2104810639730299275) [x.com](https://x.com/AquaBitcoin/status/2104811716944998590)
